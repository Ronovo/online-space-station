The Mining Bay is off the Northeast Hallway. Its station is the locker room, where a miner gets ready to mine.

For visitors

  • Buy Ore, while the Miner is on duty: the ore counter sells common ore.

The Miner Station

Miners, the Head of Personnel and the Captain can work it. It opens on the locker room:

  • Take Mining Gear: a Pickaxe and a Blast Shield from the lockers, if you don't already have them. Equip them from Hands. See {{ w.link("Items and Hands") }}.
  • Magnet and Mine Rocks: the easy way to mine, below.
  • Board the Shuttle: the dangerous way, below.

Ore sells at the {{ w.link("Cargo") }} Bay's export counter, and what you sell goes up to the station stores for the fabricator. See {{ w.link("Station Stores") }}.

The Magnet

  • Switch the magnet on and it pulls in one rock every {{ m.MAGNET_SECONDS_PER_ROCK }} seconds of game time, up to {{ m.MAGNET_MAX_ROCKS }}, while you get on with other things.
  • Mine Rocks breaks up everything it has pulled in. You need a pickaxe, carried or held.
  • Each rock gives one common ore: Iron Ore, Copper Ore or Quartz.

The Shuttle

  • The station moves over a new planet every {{ m.ORBIT_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes of game time. The shuttle takes you down to whichever one is below. You need a pickaxe, and you won't be let aboard if you're badly hurt.
  • Search for Rocks: each search takes {{ facts.mining_bay.SEARCH_SECONDS_RANGE[0] }}-{{ facts.mining_bay.SEARCH_SECONDS_RANGE[1] }} seconds of the shift and finds a vein of better ore, nothing, or a monster. Now and then it turns up an alien artifact, which the {{ w.link("Research") }} Lab pays a finder's fee for.
  • The shuttle's hold takes {{ facts.mining_bay.HOLD_CAPACITY }} ore a trip. Once it is full you can't search any more; artifacts take no room.
  • You dig some ore out of every vein you find, but you don't know what it is: it sits in the hold as unidentified ore. A Miner holding their {{ w.link("PDA") }} gets a Scan the Vein button, until the next search. Scanning names the ore, and from then on you know every vein of it on sight for the rest of the shift. There are {{ facts.surface_ores }} ores to identify, and the PDA's Ore Log keeps count.
  • Scanned or not, the station sorts and names the whole haul when the shuttle docks.
  • Return to the Station: flies you back and unloads what you dug up. The shuttle is the only way back, and it won't leave during a fight.

Combat

When a monster turns up, you fight it. There is no running away. The fight works like any other, as {{ w.link("Combat and Weapons") }} sets out.

  • A Blast Shield in your hands stops {{ m.MINING_ITEMS.blast_shield.attributes.block }}% of every hit.
  • Beating a monster turns up a piece of ore.
  • If your overall health falls below {{ k.DEFEAT_HEALTH }}%, the rescue drone flies you back to the Mining Bay, and everything you dug up on that trip, artifacts included, is lost.
