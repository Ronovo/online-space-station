What you do

You staff the station. At the HoP's Station on the Bridge you see the crew manifest, assign crew to jobs, and approve or deny job requests. See {{ w.link("Bridge and Personnel") }}.

You also have the keys to every department but Security: you can work the HoP's Station and the Bridge door, and the stations and doors of MedBay, Engineering, the Bar, the Kitchen, the Botany Lab, the Chemistry Lab, the Research Lab, Atmospherics, the Mining Bay and the Cargo Bay.

Staffing up

You start with only a Captain and Staff Assistants aboard. Use Job Assignments to give the Staff Assistants department jobs, and they head to their new posts.

Settling a request counts as a duty done. See {{ w.link("Duties and Job Events") }}. You can heal yourself and the crew at the MedBay Station. See {{ w.link("Medical") }}.

Job events

Access Request: a crew member asks to become another job. Review it at the HoP's Station Access Control, or from anywhere with the Access Control app on your {{ w.link("PDA") }}. A new request only comes in once the last one is settled.
