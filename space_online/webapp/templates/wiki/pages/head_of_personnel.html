What you do

You staff the station. At the HoP's Station on the Bridge you see the crew manifest, assign crew to jobs, and approve or deny job requests. See {{ w.link("Bridge and Personnel") }}.

You also have the keys to most departments: you can work the stations and doors of the Bridge, MedBay, Engineering, the Bar and the Botany Lab.

Staffing up

You start with only a Captain and Staff Assistants aboard. Use Job Assignments to give the Staff Assistants department jobs, and they head to their new posts. You can't make anyone Captain or move the Captain.

Job events

Access Request: a crew member asks to become another job. Review it at the HoP's Station Access Control. A new request only comes in once the last one is settled.
