What you do

You bring ore aboard and sell it at the Cargo Bay's export counter, which sends it up to the station stores. With you in the job, the fabricator only has the ore you sell. From the locker room at the Miner Station you take your gear, run the magnet, and fly the shuttle down to the planet below. You can also lock and unlock the Mining Bay door. See {{ w.link("Mining") }}.

Job events

Ore Request: a crew member asks for a common ore. Press Deliver in their room while they're on duty, for {{ e.ITEM_REQUEST_MIN_REWARD }}-{{ e.ITEM_REQUEST_MAX_REWARD }} credits. See {{ w.link("Duties and Job Events") }}.

Tips

  • Leave the magnet on. It keeps pulling rocks in while you're anywhere else on the station.
  • Hold the Pickaxe in one hand and the Blast Shield in the other before you board the shuttle.
  • Block, then Attack: the counter-attack hits harder and you take less on the way.
  • Fly back before you're badly hurt. A rescue costs you the whole trip's ore.
  • Each search takes {{ facts.mining_bay.SEARCH_SECONDS_RANGE[0] }}-{{ facts.mining_bay.SEARCH_SECONDS_RANGE[1] }} seconds, and the hold takes {{ facts.mining_bay.HOLD_CAPACITY }} ore a trip.
  • Don't sell an ore you've been asked to deliver.
  • An alien artifact from a planet is worth a finder's fee at the Research Lab.
  • Use your PDA on a vein you don't recognise to scan it. It only takes one hand, which leaves the other for the Pickaxe or the Blast Shield. Its Ore Log lists what you have identified, and it messages you when the ore store runs low. See {{ w.link("PDA") }}.
