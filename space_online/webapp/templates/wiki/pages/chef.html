What you do

You run the Kitchen. At the Chef Station you cook dishes into the kitchen's stock, read the recipe book, and plate from the food menu, which is free for you. You also pass ingredients to and from the Bar through the service window, and you can lock and unlock the Kitchen door. See {{ w.link("Kitchen") }}.

Job events

  • Food Order: a crew member orders a dish. Cook it, then press Serve at the Chef Station to hand it over for the dish's menu price. See {{ w.link("Duties and Job Events") }}.
  • Window Request: the Bartender asks for an ingredient at the service window. Put it on the window when you can. It's a favour, so it pays nothing and never runs out.

Tips

  • Keep a few dishes in stock so you can serve orders straight away.
  • Dishes that need beer, wine or rum need the Bartender to pass it through the window, from wherever they are. While they're jailed or badly hurt, what you ask for waits, and it comes through once they can work. See {{ w.link("Kitchen") }}.
  • Tomato, potato, carrot and apple come from Botany. Watch how many are left before you promise a dish.
  • Your PDA reads whether an item can be eaten and what eating it does. See {{ w.link("PDA") }}.
