What you do

You work the station's science labs. Each shift you are posted to one of them, and the first line of the station log says which. NPC Scientists hold the others. You can work every science station and lock and unlock every science lab door, but the work of your own lab is yours: no NPC does it for you.

  • Chemistry Lab: mix compounds at the chemical dispenser, break items down in the processor, look formulas up in the formula book, and send remedies to MedBay. Posted here, MedBay only has the remedies you send it. See {{ w.link("Chemistry") }} and {{ w.link("Station Stores") }}.
  • Research Lab: in the Artifact Section you find out what alien artifacts are and what sets them off, and in the Teleporter Room you run the teleporter once you have worked out this shift's numbers for it. See {{ w.link("Research") }}.
  • Atmospherics: fill canisters at the gas dispenser, move gas between canisters and Gas Tanks, heat and cool it, and burn mixes in the combustion chamber to see what they do. Posted here, you also look after the station's air: the life support reservoir and the Air Monitor. See {{ w.link("Atmospherics") }}.

Job events

Your job events come from the lab you are posted to.

  • Compound Request (Chemistry Lab): a crew member asks for a compound, such as the Doctor wanting a burn salve. Mix it and press Deliver in their room while they're on duty, for {{ e.ITEM_REQUEST_MIN_REWARD }}-{{ e.ITEM_REQUEST_MAX_REWARD }} credits.
  • Artifact Delivery (Research Lab): Central Command sends an artifact up to the bench. Work out what it is and what sets it off, and file the analysis for {{ facts.research.ANALYSIS_REWARD }} credits. None comes while the bench is full.
  • Gas Request (Atmospherics): the Doctor wants a Gas Tank of Oxygen, the Engineer one of Nitrogen, or the Botanist one of Carbon Dioxide. Fill a tank at the Canister Rack, at least {{ (facts.atmos.REQUEST_PURITY * 100)|round|int }}% that gas and {{ facts.atmos.REQUEST_PRESSURE|int }} kPa, and press Deliver in their room while they're on duty, for {{ e.ITEM_REQUEST_MIN_REWARD }}-{{ e.ITEM_REQUEST_MAX_REWARD }} credits.
  • Bomb Test (Atmospherics): Central Command wants a Small, Medium or Large blast tested. Build a transfer valve bomb of that size and run it on the Test Range, for {% for size, credits in facts.bombs.RANGE_BOUNTY.items() %}{{ credits }}{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} credits by size. See {{ w.link("Atmospherics") }}.

Tips

  • Several compounds need things the dispenser doesn't have. Aloe and produce come from {{ w.link("Botany") }}, spirits and juice from the {{ w.link("Bar") }}, and iron ore from the Miner in the {{ w.link("Mining") }} Bay.
  • Aloe Extract, Starch, Ethanol, Fruit Acid and Iron Powder come from putting other items through the processor or into the dispenser. The {{ w.link("Chemistry") }} page lists what turns into what.
  • Miracle Grow needs nothing but the dispenser, so it's the quickest request to fill.
  • Try each rig at a low strength first. Only the right one gets any reaction, and a low strength can't overload the artifact.
  • You can only tell what an artifact is once it has come alive, so set it off before you file.
  • Calibrate the teleporter early: two beacon sends give you both formulas. After that the whole station is one step away, and a Healer or a Warper in your pocket is worth keeping.
  • The Tank Rack in Atmospherics hands out an empty Gas Tank whenever you aren't carrying one, so drop or deliver a full one before you take another.
  • Your PDA reads an item's chemistry, and the gauge of a Gas Tank. Posted to the Chemistry Lab, it also messages you when MedBay is nearly out of remedies. Posted to Atmospherics, it messages you when the air goes bad or the reservoir runs low. See {{ w.link("PDA") }}.
