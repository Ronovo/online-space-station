Everything aboard runs off one battery. It drains as the station's systems draw power, and the solar array charges it back up.

Systems

  • Four systems draw power: Life Support, Hallway Lighting, Security Systems and the Communication Array. Each runs at a level from 0 (off) to 10, and higher levels draw more.
  • Life support keeps the crew breathing. See {{ w.link("Life Support") }}.
  • Hallway lighting sets how bright the station is. When the battery runs low, the lights dim on their own, and near empty they drop to red emergency lighting.

The battery

  • The solar array charges the battery by {{ facts.power.SOLAR_CHARGE_RATE|int }}% a minute while it's on.
  • At {{ facts.power.BATTERY_LOW }}% or less the battery is low, and at {{ facts.power.BATTERY_CRITICAL }}% or less it's critical, with a warning to switch on the solar array. At 0% the station has a power outage.

With the solar array on, Balanced holds the battery steady, High Performance still loses a little, and Emergency Only charges fastest.

Who controls it

The Engineering Panel, at the Engineering Station, has the solar switch, a level slider for each system and the power modes. Engineers, the Head of Personnel and the Captain can use it. See {{ w.link("Engineering") }}.
