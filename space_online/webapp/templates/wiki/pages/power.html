Everything aboard runs off one battery. It drains as the station's systems draw power. The Combustion Engine in the Engineering Bay is the main charge, and the solar array is the backup.

Systems

  • Four systems draw power: Life Support, Hallway Lighting, Security Systems and the Communication Array. Each runs at a level from 0 (off) to 10, and higher levels draw more.
  • Life support keeps the crew breathing: its level sets how fast the station's air is refreshed from the reservoir. See {{ w.link("Life Support") }}.
  • Hallway lighting sets how bright the station is. At {{ facts.lighting.DIM_LIGHTING_BATTERY }}% battery or less the lights dim on their own, and at {{ facts.lighting.EMERGENCY_LIGHTING_BATTERY }}% or less they drop to red emergency lighting.
  • Security Systems hold the door locks and flag the wanted. With them off, every locked door opens and nobody is arrested on a warrant. See {{ w.link("Security and Jail") }}.
  • The Communication Array carries station-wide announcements and calls. With it off, shuttle calls, incidents, hazards the station rolls and the crew's reports never reach the station log, the PA gives only static, and nobody can be called back to their post. Warnings about what happens to you or around you still reach the log, and the job events for your own job still arrive on your PDA and in the log.

The battery

  • Each minute the battery gains what the solar array and the Combustion Engine put out and loses what the systems draw.
  • The Combustion Engine puts out up to {{ facts.engine.OUTPUT_CAP|int }}% a minute, depending on what it burns and how well it is tuned. See {{ w.link("Engineering") }}.
  • The solar array puts out {{ facts.power.SOLAR_CHARGE_RATE }}% a minute while it's on.
  • At {{ facts.power.BATTERY_LOW }}% or less the battery is low, and at {{ facts.power.BATTERY_CRITICAL }}% or less it's critical, with a warning to check the power sources: "{{ facts.power.LOW_POWER_ALERT }}" At 0% the station has a power outage and life support shuts down with it: "{{ facts.power.POWER_OUTAGE_ALERT }}" It can't be brought back until the battery has some charge. See {{ w.link("Life Support") }}.
