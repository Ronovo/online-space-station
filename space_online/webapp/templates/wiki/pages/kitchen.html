The Kitchen is next door to the {{ w.link("Bar") }}, and the two share a service window.

For visitors

  • Order Food, while the Chef is on duty: the food menu has ready-made basics plus any dishes the kitchen has in stock. Food goes into your inventory to eat whenever you like, and a cooked dish fills you far more than a basic. The Chef crew member keeps a few dishes ready. See {{ w.link("Station Stores") }}.

The Chef Station

Chefs, the Head of Personnel and the Captain can work it.

  • Cook Dishes: put up to {{ facts.dishes.MAX_PAN_INGREDIENTS }} ingredients from the pantry in the pan and cook. If they match a recipe, the dish goes into the kitchen's stock and onto the menu. If not, you're told what went wrong. {{ facts.stores.KITCHEN_PRODUCE.values()|join(", ") }} are not in the pantry: they come from Botany through the station stores and show under Botany, with how many are left. When they run out you can buy more at the Botanist's Produce Stand in the {{ w.link("Botany") }} Lab, then stock the kitchen with them. See {{ w.link("Station Stores") }}.
  • Stock the Kitchen: send the produce you carry to the kitchen's stores, where the stove can use it.
  • View Recipes: the recipe book, by category, with a search by name, ingredient or description.
  • Serve Food: the food menu. Food is free for the Chef.
  • Service Window: see below.

The service window

Each room has ingredients the other one doesn't. The pantry is the only place to get celery, which a Bloody Mary needs, and the bar is the only place to get the beer, wine and rum that some dishes need.

  • Pass Through: choose one of your own ingredients and an amount, up to {{ facts.window_max }}, and put it on the window, where it waits for the other room. Their station can then use exactly that many. If their crew member asked for it, they take what they asked for first.
  • Ask For: choose one of the other room's ingredients and an amount. Their crew member passes it through from wherever they are. While they can't work, because they're in jail or too hurt, your request waits, and it is filled once they can.
  • The window shows what's waiting for you, what you've put out, and anything the other side is asking for.
  • What's on the window shows up in your stove or mixer under Window, with how many are left.
  • Passing things through is a favour between the two rooms. Nobody is paid for it.

Food orders

As a Chef, your job brings you crew members' orders for dishes. Cook one, then serve it from the Chef Station for the dish's menu price. See {{ w.link("Chef") }}.
