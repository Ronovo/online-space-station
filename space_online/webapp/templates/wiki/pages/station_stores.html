When the crew hold the jobs

A crew member at their post gets on with their work without being asked. Each rate below holds only while they are at their post and able to work: it stops while they are away, jailed, too hurt, or their room has a hazard, except the Engineer, who stays to fix it.

  • The Miner sends up {{ k.ORE_PER_TURN }} ore every {{ k.MINER_WORK_SECONDS }} seconds.
  • The Botanist sends the kitchen one piece of whatever it is shortest of every {{ k.BOTANIST_WORK_SECONDS }} seconds. They also grow produce in the Botany planters as a player would: they plant, water and harvest every turn, and the harvest goes to the kitchen. Visitors can see those plants in View Plants. When the Botanist is away, they may page you to water a thirsty planter.
  • The Scientist posted in Chemistry sends MedBay a remedy every {{ k.CHEMISTRY_WORK_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes while at their post.
  • The Chef cooks a dish every {{ k.CHEF_WORK_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes, using produce from the store, and the Bartender mixes a drink every {{ k.BARTENDER_WORK_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes. Each keeps up to {{ k.MAX_READY }} of a kind on the menu.
  • The Doctor treats one crew member who is too hurt to work every {{ k.DOCTOR_WORK_SECONDS }} seconds, which uses a remedy.
  • The Engineer deals with one thing every {{ k.ENGINEER_WORK_SECONDS }} seconds: a hazard, then a faulted system, then a solar array that is down, then life support a dead battery shut off, then the Combustion Engine: a cut coolant line, an overheating engine to shut down, or a stopped one to light, then hallway lighting an antagonist dimmed, which they restore and report to Security, then a nearly empty canister, which they send back down the tube to Atmospherics. See {{ w.link("Engineering") }}.
  • The Scientist posted in the Research Lab works out one artifact from the bench every {{ k.RESEARCH_WORK_SECONDS }} seconds.
  • The Scientist posted in Atmospherics looks after the station's air every {{ k.ATMOSPHERICS_WORK_SECONDS }} seconds. See {{ w.link("Atmospherics") }}.

When you hold the job

Nobody does your job for you. If you are the Miner and send up no ore, the fabricator runs dry. If you are the Scientist posted to Chemistry and send no remedies, MedBay can't treat anyone, you included. A Scientist is posted to one lab a shift, and only that lab's work is left to them: the Scientists in the other labs carry on.

  • Miner: sell ore at the {{ w.link("Cargo") }} Bay's export counter.
  • Botanist: Send Produce to the Kitchen at the Botany Station.
  • Scientist: Send Remedies to MedBay at the Chemistry Station. Burn Salve, Antitoxin, Oxygen Tonic and Healing Gel all count.
  • Anything for a store that you deliver to its room on a duty goes into the store as well.

When a post is empty

A crew member who is jailed, too badly hurt to work, or away from their post does no work. If they are jailed or hurt, the Captain covers their post from the Bridge, and failing that the Head of Personnel covers the rooms they have access to. A hazard in a room stops its work whoever is covering, except in Engineering. See {{ w.link("Crew") }} and {{ w.link("Hazards") }}.

The Captain's {{ w.link("Station Status") }} shows what is in each store.
