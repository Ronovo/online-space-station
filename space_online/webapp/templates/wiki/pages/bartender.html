What you do

You run the Bar. At the Bartender Station you mix drinks into the bar's stock, read the recipe book, and pour from the drink menu, which is free for you. You can also lock and unlock the Bar door. See {{ w.link("Bar") }}.

Job events

Drink Order: a crew member orders a mixed drink. Mix it, then press Serve at the Bartender Station to hand it over for the drink's menu price. See {{ w.link("Errands and Job Events") }}.

Tips

  • Keep a few mixed drinks in stock so you can serve orders straight away.
  • Free drinks still count toward your alcohol level. See {{ w.link("Health") }}.
