What you do

You keep the station supplied. At the Cargo Station you order supplies up from Central Command, collect them when their pod docks, and sell the station's ore, produce and artifacts at the export counter. You can also lock and unlock the Cargo Bay door. See {{ w.link("Cargo") }}.

Job events

Supply Request: a department asks for a supply, such as the Doctor wanting a medkit. Order it, collect it, and press Deliver in their room while they're on duty, for {{ e.ITEM_REQUEST_MIN_REWARD }}-{{ e.ITEM_REQUEST_MAX_REWARD }} credits. See {{ w.link("Duties and Job Events") }}.

The requests also wait under Supply Requests on the Order Console at the Cargo Station:

  • Approve orders the supply at your price. The request stays on your Duties board until you collect the supply and deliver it for the reward.
  • Deny turns it down and takes it off the board, counted as a duty done. You are paid nothing.

Your Order Console also sells canisters of dangerous gas, which go down the tube to Atmospherics, on the record. In other jobs you put in requests at the crew's Order Console instead, which an NPC Quartermaster answers. See {{ w.link("Cargo") }}.

Tips

  • A supply pod takes {{ facts.cargo.CARGO_DELIVERY_SECONDS }} seconds, and a duty runs out after {{ facts.duties.DUTY_EXPIRY_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes. Order as soon as the request comes in.
  • Your Order Console charges half what the supply counter and the crew's Order Console do, so what you order for yourself is cheap.
  • Your PDA reads an item's order, counter and export prices, and messages you when a supply pod docks. See {{ w.link("PDA") }}.
