What you do

You grow the station's food and medicine. At the Botany Station you buy seeds and supplies from the vending machine, plant and water the planters, harvest what grows, and send produce to the Kitchen. With you in the job, the stove only has the produce you send it. See {{ w.link("Station Stores") }}. You can also lock and unlock the Botany Lab door. See {{ w.link("Botany") }}.

Job events

Crop Request: the Bartender asks for a piece of produce you carry or have growing in a planter you planted. Press Deliver at the Bar while the Bartender is on duty, for {{ e.CROP_REQUEST_MIN_REWARD }}-{{ e.CROP_REQUEST_MAX_REWARD }} credits. A request for a plant still growing lasts until it is ripe, then {{ facts.duties.DUTY_EXPIRY_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes more. With nothing in hand or growing, no request comes. See {{ w.link("Duties and Job Events") }}.

Tips

  • Water every plant before its stage ends. You're told when a plant dies, and Station Status counts the thirsty ones.
  • Aloe soothes burns. Keep a leaf or two for hallway accidents.
  • The Ooze gives a plant a chance of a mutant harvest, which sells for far more at the Cargo Bay.
  • Use your PDA on a planter to read the plant, and it messages you when the kitchen runs out of a kind of produce. See {{ w.link("PDA") }}.
