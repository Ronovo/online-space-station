What you do

You grow the station's food and medicine. At the Botany Station you buy seeds and supplies from the vending machine, plant and water the planters, and harvest what grows. You can also lock and unlock the Botany Lab door. See {{ w.link("Botany") }}.

Job events

Crop Request: the Bartender asks for a piece of produce. Grow it, or buy it, and press Deliver at the Bar while the Bartender is on duty, for {{ e.CROP_REQUEST_MIN_REWARD }}-{{ e.CROP_REQUEST_MAX_REWARD }} credits. See {{ w.link("Errands and Job Events") }}.

Tips

  • Water every plant before its stage ends. You're told when a plant dies, and Station Status counts the thirsty ones.
  • Crops grow fastest, so they're the quickest way to fill a request.
  • Aloe soothes burns. Keep a leaf or two for hallway accidents.
