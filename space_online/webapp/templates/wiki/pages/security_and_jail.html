Warrants and fines

  • A warrant means someone is wanted and will be arrested when Security catches them.
  • A fine is owed credits. When a fined crew member meets a Security Guard, the guard collects it. If they can't pay, or refuse (about {{ (j.FINE_REFUSE_CHANCE * 100)|round|int }}% of the time), they're jailed instead.
  • Warrants go out when a Security Guard witnesses a crime, when crew commit petty crimes, when the Captain approves one, when Security issues one at the station, when someone forces a door that isn't theirs to open, when someone sets off a bomb on the station with no revealed antagonist loose (see {{ w.link("Atmospherics") }}), or when someone orders more than {{ facts.cargo.DANGEROUS_ORDERS_ALLOWED }} canisters of dangerous gas in a shift (a {{ (facts.cargo.ORDER_ALERT_CHANCE * 100)|round|int }}% chance for each one past that). See {{ w.link("Cargo") }}.
  • Warrants are only acted on while the Security Systems have power. See {{ w.link("Power") }}.

The Security Station

Security Guards and the Captain can work it.

  • View Crew Manifest: the whole crew by department, marking anyone wanted or jailed.
  • Issue Warrant/Fine: issue a warrant or a fine, with a reason, or clear a warrant. You can't put out a warrant for yourself. A fine is a whole number of credits from 1 to {{ j.MAX_FINE }}, and nobody owes more than that in all.
  • View Jail: everyone locked up and how long they have left. Pardon releases a prisoner, and Add Time adds {{ j.ADD_TIME_SECONDS // 60 }} minute to a sentence.
  • Incident Log: everything the antagonists have done this shift, with who was away from their post each time. See {{ w.link("Antagonists") }}. Every order of dangerous gas through Cargo is there too, with who ordered it and when.

Arrests

  • As a Security Guard or the Captain, you scan a room for warrants when you walk in, and get the choice to Arrest or Let Go wanted crew you meet in rooms or halls, or call back to their post. Some will offer a bribe of {{ j.BRIBE_MIN_CREDITS }}-{{ j.BRIBE_MAX_CREDITS }} credits to be let go, which clears their warrant. Nobody offers one over a warrant you wrote yourself, and an antagonist never does.
  • Security Guard crew arrest wanted crew they find on their patrols, including you.
  • Station security bots can arrest you too if you're wanted.

Jail

A sentence lasts {{ j.DEFAULT_SENTENCE_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes. While jailed you're held in the Security Office and can't leave or walk the halls until you're released or pardoned. Being jailed for a fine settles the fine. A hidden antagonist who is jailed is held for Central Command: no release and no pardon.

The crew shuttle has a cell of its own. Security Guards and the Captain arrest the wanted crew aboard into it with Scan the Passengers, and the station's prisoners join it when the shuttle leaves. Nobody is let out of it, and a prisoner rides in it all the way to Central Command, you included. See {{ w.link("Rounds and the Shuttle") }}.

The Armory

Whoever can work the Security Station can take its kit from the Armory: {{ facts.armory|join(", ") }}. The baton and the handcuffs are for antagonists. See {{ w.link("Combat and Weapons") }} and {{ w.link("Antagonists") }}.
