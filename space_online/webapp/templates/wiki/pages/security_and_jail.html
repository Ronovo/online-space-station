Warrants and fines

  • A warrant means someone is wanted and will be arrested when Security catches them.
  • A fine is owed credits. When a fined crew member meets a Security Guard, the guard collects it. If they can't pay, or refuse (about {{ (j.FINE_REFUSE_CHANCE * 100)|round|int }}% of the time), they're jailed instead.
  • Warrants go out when a Security Guard witnesses a crime, when crew commit petty crimes, when the Captain approves one, or when Security issues one at the station.

The Security Station

Security Guards and the Captain can work it.

  • View Crew Manifest: the whole crew by department, marking anyone wanted or jailed.
  • Issue Warrant/Fine: issue a warrant or a fine to anyone, with a reason, or clear a warrant.
  • View Jail: everyone locked up and how long they have left. Pardon releases a prisoner, and Add Time adds {{ j.ADD_TIME_SECONDS // 60 }} minute to a sentence.

Arrests

  • As a Security Guard or the Captain, you scan a room for warrants when you walk in, and get the choice to Arrest or Let Go wanted crew you meet in rooms or halls, or call back to their post. Some will offer a bribe of {{ j.BRIBE_MIN_CREDITS }}-{{ j.BRIBE_MAX_CREDITS }} credits to be let go, which clears their warrant.
  • Security Guard crew arrest wanted crew they find on their patrols, including you.
  • Station security bots can arrest you too if you're wanted.

Jail

A sentence lasts {{ j.DEFAULT_SENTENCE_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes. While jailed you're held in the Security office and can't leave or walk the halls until you're released or pardoned.
