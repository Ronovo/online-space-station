Who's aboard

The crew is made up around your job. Every department post other than yours gets a crew member posted at its room, and a couple of Staff Assistants wander the halls. A post is a job in a room: most jobs have one, and the Scientist has one in each science lab. A Scientist player is posted to one lab and finds Scientist crew in the others. On top of that come {{ facts.extra_crew[0] }} or {{ facts.extra_crew[1] }} extra crew in jobs picked at random, never yours and never the Captain's or the Head of Personnel's. They share a job with its post holder but spend the shift roaming.

On duty and away

  • When the crew shuttle is called, the crew away from their posts head for Departures, and the rest follow once it docks. See {{ w.link("Rounds and the Shuttle") }}.
  • Posted crew stay at their post and get on with their work. See {{ w.link("Station Stores") }}. Now and then one leaves on an errand to another room and comes straight back, or just goes for a walk. The Captain and the Security Guard patrol more.
  • While someone is on duty, their room offers their service: the Bartender takes orders, the Doctor treats you, and so on.
  • When they're away, the room shows a Call button. They answer about {{ (facts.call_chance * 100)|round|int }}% of the time and head back to their post.
  • A jailed crew member can't be called. The room shows when they get out.
  • Nobody can be called while the Communication Array is down. See {{ w.link("Power") }}.

Too hurt to work

  • A crew member whose overall health falls into the Critical band stops working and stops moving until a Doctor treats them. Their room says so.
  • The Doctor crew member treats them in time, while MedBay has remedies. See {{ w.link("Medical") }}.
  • While a post holder is jailed or too hurt, the Captain covers their work, and failing that the Head of Personnel covers the rooms they have access to.

Crew life

  • Wandering crew pick up small injuries and heal from them. A Doctor can treat them. See {{ w.link("Medical") }}.
  • Now and then someone commits a petty crime and a warrant goes out. Security Guards and Captains never do. See {{ w.link("Security and Jail") }}.
