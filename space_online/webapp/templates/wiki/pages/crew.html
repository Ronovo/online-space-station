Who's aboard

The crew is made up around your job. Every department job other than yours gets a crew member posted at their room, and a couple of Staff Assistants wander the halls. A Head of Personnel starts with only a Captain and Staff Assistants, and fills the other jobs. See {{ w.link("Bridge and Personnel") }}.

On duty and away

  • Posted crew sometimes leave their room. They wander the halls, visit another room for a while, then head back.
  • While someone is on duty, their room offers their service: the Bartender takes orders, the Doctor treats you, and so on.
  • When they're away, the room shows a Call button. They answer about {{ (facts.call_chance * 100)|round|int }}% of the time and head back to their post.
  • A jailed crew member can't be called. The room shows when they get out.

Crew life

  • Wandering crew pick up small injuries and heal from them. A Doctor can treat them. See {{ w.link("Medical") }}.
  • Now and then someone commits a petty crime and a warrant goes out. Security Guards and Captains never do. See {{ w.link("Security and Jail") }}.
  • The Crew Manifest at the Security and Bridge stations lists everyone by department.
