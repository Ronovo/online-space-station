Career rounds and Antagonist single rounds have one to {{ t.MAX_THREATS }} antagonists, each a different kind, and each starting at its own time. Peaceful rounds have none.

How a threat plays out

  • In secret: at first you only see what it does: a sabotaged system, crew found hurt, missing credits. Some antagonists are crew members in disguise, and nothing says who.
  • Revealed: after a few moves it attacks someone in the open. Now everyone knows who or what it is. A crew member who turns out to be one leaves the crew.
  • Hunted: a revealed antagonist roams the hallways. The status bar says where it was last seen. If it is on your hallway tile, it attacks you. Rooms are safe.
  • Dealt with: you can beat it in a fight, or the crew can. Security Guards and the Captain go after a revealed antagonist on their own and wear it down, getting hurt in the process.

Catching one before it strikes

  • Every move an antagonist makes goes down in the Incident Log, at the Security Station and the Captain's Station: when, where, what happened, and which crew were away from their posts at the time.
  • An antagonist hiding as a crew member has to leave its post to do anything, so it is on that list every time. Whoever is away for every incident is your suspect. The longer you wait the shorter the list, and after {{ t.REVEAL_AFTER_RANGE[0] }} or {{ t.REVEAL_AFTER_RANGE[1] }} moves it reveals itself anyway.
  • Put a warrant out for them and arrest them, or let a Security Guard find them. Jailing the right crew member ends that antagonist on the spot, and they are held for Central Command. See {{ w.link("Security and Jail") }}.
  • An antagonist from outside the crew can't be caught this way, and neither can one that is too hurt to move. If an antagonist is secretly one of the crew, jailing that crew member catches it.

Fighting one

The fight works like any other. See {{ w.link("Combat and Weapons") }}. Lose it and you die, which ends the round.

When the shuttle comes

  • Once the shuttle docks, an antagonist in the open stops roaming and makes for the Departures door. It still attacks you if it catches you in a hallway on the way.
  • It is aboard only if it has reached that door when the shuttle leaves. One hiding as a crew member is aboard only if that crew member boarded. One from outside that never showed itself stays on the station. Any that don't make it are left behind, and the shift report says so.
  • Each antagonist aboard makes its move in turn, and it goes for anyone aboard: you or any crew member, picked at random.
  • If it goes for a crew member, it kills them. The rest of the crew fight back: it loses {{ (t.SHUTTLE_CREW_HIT * 100)|round|int }}% of its full health for every crew member aboard who can fight, up to {{ (t.SHUTTLE_CREW_HIT_MAX * 100)|round|int }}%. Then it picks again. Enough crew bring it down before it ever reaches you.
  • If it goes for you, you fight it with whatever health it has left. If you were the first it went for, that is all of it.
  • So a full shuttle is the safest place to be: it is less likely to pick you, and every crew member it goes through costs it more.
