Station Status is the Captain's briefing on the whole station, opened from the Captain's Station on the Bridge. See {{ w.link("Bridge and Personnel") }}.

Overall condition

  • NOMINAL: nothing needs attention.
  • CAUTION: something does, like a low battery, injured crew, a system fault, a locked room, wanted or jailed crew, unpaid fines, a pending request or errand, or plants that need water or have died.
  • CRITICAL: a critical battery or outage, an oxygen emergency, crew in critical condition, or an emergency announcement.

Sections

  • Alerts: every current problem, critical ones in red.
  • Power and systems: battery level, power mode, the solar array, and each system's status and level. See {{ w.link("Power") }}.
  • Crew: how many are aboard, on duty or away, healthy, injured or critical, and suffering from low oxygen.
  • Security: whether security systems are online, and everyone wanted, jailed or fined. See {{ w.link("Security and Jail") }}.
  • Facilities: locked rooms, and the Botany planters in use, mature, needing water and dead. See {{ w.link("Botany") }}.
