The lobby is where every round starts. Your account keeps one career, and you can play a single round between career shifts without touching it. Every round, in every mode, ends with the crew shuttle. See {{ w.link("Rounds and the Shuttle") }}.

A single round is a throwaway character. It starts with its job's own credits and is not paid a salary. You can abandon one from the lobby; a career shift has to be seen through.

The career ladder

A career starts as a {{ c.START_JOB }} with {{ facts.start_credits }} credits and no other job to its name. A job you have unlocked is one you can start a shift in from then on.

  • End a shift holding a job and it is unlocked. Ask the Head of Personnel for it at Access Control on the Bridge. See {{ w.link("Bridge and Personnel") }}.
  • A shift that ends without a new job, while there are jobs left to unlock, lets you pick one from the shift report or the lobby.
  • {{ c.HEAD_JOB }} unlocks once every other job is unlocked, whichever way you got them.
  • {{ c.TOP_JOB }} unlocks after a shift you started as {{ c.HEAD_JOB }}.

What carries over

  • Your credits and your unlocked jobs. Shares are sold at the end of every shift and the money added to your credits.
  • The files you wrote on your computer. See {{ w.link("PDA") }}.
  • Nothing else. Every shift you arrive on a fresh station, with a new crew, a new locker, empty hands and full health.
  • The salary is paid for the job you hold when payday comes, so changing to a better-paid job mid-shift pays better.

Dying in a career

  • Dying ends the round on the spot. The round still counts: it unlocks the job you died in, or leaves you a pick.
  • You are revived for {{ c.REVIVE_BASE_COST }} credits the first time, and {{ c.REVIVE_COST_STEP }} more each time after that.
  • If you can't pay, the career is over, and you start a new one from the bottom.
  • You die by losing a fight to an antagonist, by running out of oxygen, in a Large transfer valve bomb blast, or by being left behind when the shuttle leaves. See {{ w.link("Antagonists") }}, {{ w.link("Life Support") }} and {{ w.link("Atmospherics") }}.
