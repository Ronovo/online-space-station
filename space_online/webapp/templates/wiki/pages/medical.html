MedBay for visitors

  • Request Health Check: a free scan of your limbs, damage and alcohol, with what treatment would cost.
  • Talk to Doctor, while the Doctor is on duty: they examine you and offer treatment. Paying heals every limb, clears all burn, poison and oxygen damage, and sobers you up if you need it. It costs, per point of damage: {% for kind, multiplier in facts.cost_multipliers.items() %}{{ kind }} {{ multiplier }}{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} credits.
  • Every treatment in MedBay uses one remedy from the station stores, which the Scientist in Chemistry keeps filled. With none left, nobody can be treated. See {{ w.link("Station Stores") }}.

The MedBay Station

Doctors, the Head of Personnel and the Captain can work it. It shows how many remedies are in stock and, while someone is making them, when the next one comes. If the Scientist posted in Chemistry is away from their post, you can call them back from here.

  • Heal Injuries: free, full treatment for yourself: every limb and every kind of damage.
  • Check Crew Vitals: everyone's health at a glance, with limb and damage details. An injured crew member has a Treat button. Treating them heals them completely, and they pay you the treatment cost from their own credits, or what they have if they can't afford it all.

Patients

As a Doctor, your job brings you crew members hurt in accidents. Treat them from Crew Vitals to finish the duty. Crew who are too hurt to work also wait for you. See {{ w.link("Doctor") }} and {{ w.link("Duties and Job Events") }}.
