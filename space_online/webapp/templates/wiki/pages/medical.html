MedBay for visitors

  • Request Health Check: a free scan of your limbs, damage and alcohol, with what treatment would cost.
  • Talk to Doctor, while the Doctor is on duty: they examine you and offer treatment. Paying heals every limb, clears all burn, poison and oxygen damage, and sobers you up if you need it. The MedBay Station

Doctors, the Head of Personnel and the Captain can work it.

  • Heal Injuries: free, full treatment for yourself: every limb and every kind of damage.
  • Check Crew Vitals: everyone's health at a glance, with limb and damage details. An injured crew member has a Treat button. Treating them heals them completely, and they pay you the treatment cost from their own credits, or what they have if they can't afford it all.

Patients

As a Doctor, a hallway job event can tell you a crew member was hurt in an accident. Treat them from Crew Vitals to finish the errand. See {{ w.link("Doctor") }} and {{ w.link("Errands and Job Events") }}.
