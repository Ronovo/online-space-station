Life support is the power system that keeps oxygen flowing to the whole crew, you included.

When it fails

  • At life support level {{ o.DAMAGE_THRESHOLD_LEVEL }} or below, everyone aboard takes oxygen damage. The lower the level, the faster it builds, up to twice as fast at 0.
  • Switching life support off sends a station-wide emergency announcement. Damage holds off for {{ o.OXYGEN_DEPLETION_GRACE_SECONDS }} seconds, so there's time to turn it back on.
  • You're warned as your oxygen damage passes {{ o.WARNING_THRESHOLDS|join("%, ") }}%.
  • At {{ o.DEATH_THRESHOLD }}% you pass out, and emergency systems revive you in your Quarters badly hurt. See {{ w.link("Health") }}.

Recovering

Once life support is back above level {{ o.DAMAGE_THRESHOLD_LEVEL }}, oxygen damage wears off by 1% every {{ o.RECOVERY_SECONDS_PER_PERCENT }} seconds, but only down to {{ o.DOCTOR_REQUIRED_FLOOR }}% if it was that high or higher. Past that point, a Doctor has to treat it. See {{ w.link("Medical") }}.

Keeping it running

Life support is set from the Engineering Panel and drains the battery like any other system. See {{ w.link("Power") }}. The Captain may also be asked to approve diverting power to it. See {{ w.link("Captain") }}.
