Life support keeps the station's air fresh for the whole crew, you included. The station has one air, shared by every room and hallway.

How the air works

  • Everyone aboard breathes Oxygen into Carbon Dioxide, all the time.
  • Life support's scrubbers draw the stale air off, and its pump refills the air from the reservoir in {{ w.link("Atmospherics") }}. Both run at the life support level set on the Engineering Panel: the higher the level, the faster the air is turned over.
  • At level {{ (a.KEEPS_UP_LEVEL + 1)|int }} and above the air stays fresh for a full crew. At level {{ a.KEEPS_UP_LEVEL|int }} and below the pump can't keep up: Oxygen sags and Carbon Dioxide builds until the air starts to hurt, sooner and worse the lower the level.
  • With life support off nothing is pumped or scrubbed, and a full crew breathes the air down to where it hurts in about {{ a.AIR_LASTS_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes. Switching it off sends a station-wide emergency announcement.
  • A hull breach vents the air to space and a fire burns its Oxygen. See {{ w.link("Hazards") }}.

The reservoir

The reservoir starts each shift with {{ a.RESERVOIR_FILL|round|int }} mol of air: enough for the longest shift at level 10 with half as much again, so on a quiet shift nobody needs to touch it. It runs low after hull breaches, venting or tampering. Whatever is in it is what the station's air turns into: pump Oxygen in and the air grows Oxygen-rich, while Plasma or Carbon Dioxide in the reservoir hurts the whole station. The Scientist in Atmospherics looks after it. See {{ w.link("Atmospherics") }}.

Emergency oxygen

  • Hold a Gas Tank in a hand with its valve open and you breathe from it through a mask instead of the station's air. Whatever is in the tank is what you breathe.
  • A full tank lasts about {{ a.TANK_LASTS_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes. You're told when it is down to a quarter and when it runs out, and then you are back on the station's air.
  • Open and close the valve from the tank's actions in your inventory. A tank in your inventory, or with its valve closed, does nothing. Your Character Sheet shows when you are breathing from one.
  • Fill one at the Canister Rack in {{ w.link("Atmospherics") }}.

Recovering

While the air has enough Oxygen and pressure and not too much Carbon Dioxide, oxygen damage wears off by 1% every {{ o.RECOVERY_SECONDS_PER_PERCENT }} seconds, but only down to {{ o.DOCTOR_REQUIRED_FLOOR }}% if it was that high or higher. Past that point, a Doctor has to treat it. Poison damage never wears off on its own. See {{ w.link("Medical") }}.

Keeping it running

Life support is set from the Engineering Panel and drains the battery like any other system. See {{ w.link("Power") }}. A dead battery shuts it down, and once there is charge again someone has to bring it back up: the Engineer crew member does, or you do if that is your job. The Captain may also be asked to approve diverting power to it. See {{ w.link("Captain") }}.
