What you do

You run the station. You can work every station and lock or unlock every door. Your own Captain's Station on the Bridge has the Station Status briefing, security alerts and the crew manifest. See {{ w.link("Station Status") }} and {{ w.link("Bridge and Personnel") }}.

  • Like a Security Guard, you scan rooms for warrants and can arrest wanted crew. See {{ w.link("Security and Jail") }}.
  • Through Job Assignments you're the only one who can make someone Captain or move the current Captain.

Job events

Command Request: a department asks for your approval. Approve or deny it:

  • Divert power to life support: life support goes to full power.
  • Lock down a sector: a department door is locked.
  • Switch to Power Saving mode: the station changes power mode.
  • A warrant for a suspect: a warrant goes out for that crew member.

Denying a request changes nothing.
