What you do

You run the station. You can work every station and lock or unlock every door but Departures. Your own Captain's Station on the Bridge has the Station Status briefing, security alerts, the incident log and the crew manifest. See {{ w.link("Station Status") }} and {{ w.link("Bridge and Personnel") }}.

  • Like a Security Guard, you scan rooms for warrants and can arrest wanted crew. See {{ w.link("Security and Jail") }}.
  • You call the crew shuttle and end the shift with Call Crew Shuttle at the Captain's Station, and you can recall it, at most {{ facts.rounds.SHUTTLE_RECALLS_PER_SHIFT }} recall per shift. See {{ w.link("Rounds and the Shuttle") }}.
  • Your PDA has Broadcast, which speaks to the whole station from wherever you are. See {{ w.link("PDA") }}.
  • You can heal yourself and the crew at the MedBay Station. See {{ w.link("Medical") }}.

Job events

Command Request: a department asks for your approval. It waits on your Duties board, where you approve or deny it. Either answer is a duty done, and one left unanswered runs out as missed. A request only comes when it makes sense: life support below full, an Engineer and a Security Guard aboard to carry it out, a door left to lock, a suspect to name.

  • Divert power to life support: life support goes to full power.
  • Lock down a room: the request names the room, and its door is locked.
  • Switch to Power Saving mode: the station changes power mode.
  • A warrant for a suspect: a warrant goes out for that crew member.

Denying a request changes nothing.
