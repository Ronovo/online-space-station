What a hazard does

  • It is announced in the station log, as long as the Communication Array is up, and shows in {{ w.link("Station Status") }} either way.
  • You are warned as you walk in. Every {{ h.HAZARD_HURT_SECONDS }} seconds you stay, it does {{ h.HAZARD_DAMAGE }}% damage. A hazard on its own never takes oxygen or burn damage past {{ h.HAZARD_DAMAGE_CAP }}%.
  • The room's crew clear out, so nobody serves you there and the room's work stops until it is fixed. See {{ w.link("Station Stores") }}.

Hazards and the air

The station shares one air, so a hazard in one room reaches everyone. See {{ w.link("Life Support") }}.

  • A hull breach vents {{ (facts.air.BREACH_VENT_SHARE * 100)|round|int }}% of the station's air to space every second. The pump replaces it from the reservoir, so an open breach drains the reservoir, and with life support low the whole station thins out. Sealing it stops the loss.
  • A fire burns the station's Oxygen into Carbon Dioxide. While the air is Oxygen-rich, above {{ facts.gases.OXYGEN_RICH }} kPa of Oxygen, it burns hotter and does {{ h.RICH_FIRE_DAMAGE }}% a hit.
  • Fuel in the air: once the air holds {{ facts.air.FLASH_POINT }} kPa or more of Plasma or Hydrogen, a fire or arcing wiring anywhere aboard sets it off. The air flashes into flame, everyone aboard takes {{ facts.air.FLASH_BURN }}% burns, the fuel burns away, and a fire breaks out in a room.

Fixing one

  • Stand in the room with the right tool, carried or held, and press the button at the top of its menu. Anyone can do it.
  • A Wrench and Wirecutters come in the Basic Tools set in your locker. A set counts as long as the tool is still in it. The Fire Extinguisher is made at the fabricator or bought at the {{ w.link("Cargo") }} Bay.
  • The Engineer crew member deals with one hazard at a time before anything else. If you are the Engineer, they are yours. See {{ w.link("Engineer") }}.
