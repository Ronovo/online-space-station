Space Station Explorer is a text game played in your browser. You live aboard a space station as one crew member, doing your job alongside the rest of the crew.

Your account

  • Register with a username, a password and a security question. If the game is linked to the portfolio, you can also log in with your portfolio account.
  • Forgot password? resets your password with your security answer. Five wrong answers lock resets for 15 minutes, and five wrong passwords in a row lock logins for a minute.

Your character

  • Each account has one character. You pick a name, a department and a job. Your job sets your starting credits and which station doors and controls you can use. Each job has its own page under Jobs in the guide index.
  • You start in your Quarters with a few items in your storage locker. See {{ w.link("Items and Hands") }}.
  • Your progress is saved after every action. Save and Exit takes you back to your account page, and Continue picks up where you left off. The Bed in your Quarters just confirms the save.
  • Deleting your character on the account page erases all its progress, so you can roll a new one.

The Character Sheet

Open it from any screen. It shows your job, department, credits, alcohol level, limb health and damage, and opens your inventory, your stock holdings and your notes. Notes are a log of everything that has happened to you, newest first.
