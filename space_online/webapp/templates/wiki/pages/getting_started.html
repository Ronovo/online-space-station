Space Station Explorer is a text game played in your browser. You live aboard a space station as one crew member, doing your job alongside the rest of the crew.

Your account

  • Register with a username, a password and a security question. If the game is linked to the portfolio, you can also log in with your portfolio account.
  • Forgot password? resets your password with your security answer. Five wrong answers lock resets for 15 minutes, and five wrong passwords in a row lock logins for a minute.

The lobby

  • The game is played in rounds. Each round is one shift on the station, and it ends when the crew shuttle takes everyone home. See {{ w.link("Rounds and the Shuttle") }}.
  • From the lobby you start or continue your career, which climbs a ladder of jobs, or play a single round in any job, Peaceful or with antagonists. See {{ w.link("Modes and Careers") }}.
  • Your job sets which station doors and controls you can use. Each job has its own page under Jobs in the guide index.
  • You start every round in your Quarters with a few items in your storage locker. See {{ w.link("Items and Hands") }}.
  • You also start with a PDA in your inventory. Hold it in a hand for messages from your job, the Order Console, the stock prices, your files and your job's scanner. See {{ w.link("PDA") }}.
  • Your progress is saved after every action. Save and Exit, at the right of the status bar in the hallways and your Quarters, takes you back to the lobby, and Continue picks the round up where you left off. The shift stands still while you are away: its clock only runs while the game is open.
  • Delete Career in the lobby erases your career and any round in progress.

The screen

  • The status bar across the top shows the alert level, the shift clock, your health, the crew shuttle once it has been called, and where any antagonist loose on the station was last seen.
  • The Station Log runs down the side, newest line first. Everything the game tells you goes there, and nothing stops you to make you read it. A line stays highlighted for {{ facts.log_new_seconds }} seconds.
  • When you are hurt, the edge of the screen flashes red and your health in the status bar drops.
  • A box in the middle of the screen is only ever a question the game needs answered.
  • Items lying where you stand show under the screen, each with a Pick Up button.

The Character Sheet

Open it from any screen. It shows your job, department, credits, hunger, thirst, alcohol level, limb health and damage, and opens your inventory, your stock holdings and your notes. Notes keep everything that has happened to you, newest first.
