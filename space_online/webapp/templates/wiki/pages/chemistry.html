The Chemistry Lab is the east door of the Science Hall, east of the East Section of the ring. {{ w.link("Atmospherics") }} is its north door and the {{ w.link("Research") }} Lab its south door. Visitors can talk to the Scientist posted here; the work happens at the Chemistry Station.

The Chemistry Station

Scientists, the Head of Personnel and the Captain can work it. See {{ w.link("Scientist") }}.

  • Chemical Dispenser: add up to {{ c.MAX_BEAKER_CHEMICALS }} chemicals to the beaker and mix. If they match a formula, the compound goes into your inventory. The dispenser has an endless supply of: {{ c.DISPENSER_CHEMICALS|join(", ") }}.
  • Processor: breaks a solid item you're carrying down into a chemical the beaker can take.
  • Formula Book: every compound and what goes into it.
  • Send Remedies to MedBay: sends the remedies you carry to MedBay's stock. See {{ w.link("Station Stores") }}.

Adding items from around the station

  • What the Processor makes goes straight in. It shows in the dispenser under Inventory, with how many you have, and is used up when you mix.
  • An alcoholic drink from the {{ w.link("Bar") }} goes in as Ethanol, and a juice goes in as Fruit Acid.
