Atmospherics is the north door of the Science Hall, which also leads to {{ w.link("Chemistry") }} and the {{ w.link("Research") }} Lab. It holds the station's gas canisters, a gas dispenser, a heater/freezer, a combustion chamber, and the life support reservoir that feeds the station's air. Scientists, the Head of Personnel and the Captain can work the Atmospherics Station. See {{ w.link("Scientist") }}. Visitors can talk to the Scientist posted here.

Gases

Every gauge reads a pressure in kPa, a temperature in kelvin, and each gas as a share of the whole. Pressure goes up with the amount of gas (in mol) and with its temperature. Mixing two gases settles at a temperature between theirs, weighted by how much heat each holds.

The Canister Rack

The rack has {{ a.CANISTER_SLOTS }} slots. Each canister holds {{ g.CANISTER.volume }} L and is rated to {{ g.CANISTER.rated }} kPa. A shift starts with these, each at {{ a.CANISTER_FILL }} kPa and room temperature:

    • {% endfor %}

Pick a canister to read its gauge. Then:

  • Transfer an amount in mol into another canister.
  • Fill the Tank from it, into a Gas Tank you carry, or Empty the Tank into the Canister. A tank won't be filled past its rated pressure.
  • Vent to Space empties it.
  • Send to Engineering sends it down the tube into a free slot of the Combustion Engine's canister bay, where the Engineer burns it. A canister on a port has to be disconnected first. See {{ w.link("Engineering") }}.
  • Port: connect it to the {{ a.PORTS.chamber }}, which feeds the combustion chamber, or the {{ a.PORTS.reservoir }}, which reaches the life support reservoir. One canister fits on a port; connecting another takes the first one off.
  • Heater / Freezer: set a target from {{ a.HEATER_RANGE[0] }} K to {{ a.HEATER_RANGE[1] }} K and switch it on. It moves the canister's temperature toward the target by {{ a.HEATER_WATTS // 1000 }} kW, so a full canister takes longer than a nearly empty one. There is one heater/freezer: switching it on for a canister takes it off any other.

A canister pushed past its rated pressure ruptures. Its gas is lost, Atmospherics gets a fire if the gas held a fuel and an oxidizer or a hull breach if it didn't, and the blast takes {{ a.RUPTURE_DAMAGE }}% off a limb of anyone in the room. Heating a canister of fuel and oxidizer until the fuel lights sets it burning inside the canister, which can rupture it. See {{ w.link("Hazards") }}.

Canisters are never carried. They travel by tube: from the Cargo Bay to the rack, and between the rack and the Combustion Engine's bay in the Engineering Bay.

The Gas Dispenser

Pick a gas and a canister, set an amount in mol, and dispense. The gas goes in pure at room temperature. The panel shows the canister's gauge now and after, and won't dispense past its rated pressure.

  • {% for name in g.GASES if name not in g.DANGEROUS_GASES %}{{ name }}{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}: free and unlimited.
  • {{ g.DANGEROUS_GASES|join(", ") }}: {{ a.DISPENSER_STOCK }} mol of each a shift, about two canisters' worth. The panel shows what is left.

More of the dangerous gases can be ordered from Central Command on the Order Console, as a full canister. It comes down the tube from the Cargo Bay into an empty slot on the rack, or waits there until one is free. Every order goes in the Incident Log, and ordering too many can put out a warrant for you. See {{ w.link("Cargo") }}.

The Combustion Chamber

A sealed chamber of {{ a.CHAMBER.volume }} L, rated to {{ a.CHAMBER.rated }} kPa, for burning a mix to see what it does.

  • Inject an amount in mol from the canister on the {{ a.PORTS.chamber }}.
  • Igniter: a spark. It lights the chamber if there is a fuel and an oxidizer in it.
  • A burn goes on until nothing more reacts. The log then gives its peak temperature and pressure, so you can compare one mix with the last.
  • Pump Out all of the chamber's gas, hot as it is, into a canister you pick. This is how you get hot Carbon Dioxide or leftover hot fuel into a canister.
  • Vent to Space empties the chamber and puts out the fire.
  • Past its rated pressure the chamber ruptures, like a canister.

The Reservoir

Life support pumps the station's air from the reservoir: {{ g.RESERVOIR.volume }} L, rated to {{ g.RESERVOIR.rated }} kPa. What it does to the air is on {{ w.link("Life Support") }}.

  • The panel reads its gauge and how full it is.
  • Pump In an amount in mol from the canister on the {{ a.PORTS.reservoir }}: air to top it up, Oxygen to enrich it, or something that hurts the station. It won't pump past the reservoir's rated pressure.
  • Draw Off an amount into that canister, for instance to get a contaminant back out. A canister pushed past its rating ruptures.

The Air Monitor

  • Reads the station's air, and how it stands against each breathing limit: OK, Low, Rich or Dangerous.
  • Shows the life support level and how fast the pump refills the air at it.
  • Vent the Station dumps {{ (air.VENT_SHARE * 100)|round|int }}% of the station's air to space, the way to clear a contaminant out of it. It asks first: everyone breathes thin air until the pump refills it from the reservoir, and at low life support that can kill.

The Scientist on post

Every {{ facts.work.ATMOSPHERICS_WORK_SECONDS }} seconds the Scientist posted here deals with one thing: a reservoir with more than {{ (air.RESERVOIR_TRACE * 100)|round(1) }}% of something other than air in it, which they flush with {{ air.UPKEEP_MOLES|round|int }} mol of fresh air, then a contaminant in the station's air, which they vent, then a reservoir under {{ (air.RESERVOIR_LOW * 100)|round|int }}% full, which they top up, then a canister on one of the Combustion Engine's lines that is down to under {{ facts.engine.LOW_MOLES|round|int }} mol with no spare waiting in the engine's bay, for which they fill a canister on the rack with Hydrogen or Oxygen from the Gas Dispenser and send it down the tube. They pick the emptiest canister that holds nothing but that gas, often one the Engineer sent back, and Hydrogen comes out of the shift's stock, so with the stock run down nothing goes. If you hold the post, it is yours: your PDA messages you when the air goes bad or the reservoir runs low.

Gas Tanks and the Gas Analyzer

  • The Tank Rack at the Atmospherics Station hands out an empty Gas Tank and a Gas Analyzer, if you aren't carrying one already.
  • A Gas Tank holds {{ g.GAS_TANK.volume }} L and is rated to {{ g.GAS_TANK.rated }} kPa. Examining it reads its pressure. What is in it takes the Gas Analyzer, or a Scientist's PDA.
  • Open a tank's valve and hold it in a hand to breathe from it. See {{ w.link("Life Support") }}.
  • Use the Gas Analyzer to read the station's air and every Gas Tank you carry or hold. The fabricator in the {{ w.link("Engineering") }} Bay makes more for {{ facts.fabrication_ore["Gas Analyzer"] }} ore.

Transfer valve bombs

A Tank Transfer Valve joins two Gas Tanks. Opening it pours their gas together, and a fuel that is already hot enough to light burns with the oxidizer on the other side. The burn's heat drives the pressure past the tanks' rating, and they burst. The same gas rules as the canisters and the combustion chamber decide everything.

  • Getting a valve: the Tank Rack hands out one free valve a shift. The fabricator in the {{ w.link("Engineering") }} Bay makes more for {{ facts.fabrication_ore["Tank Transfer Valve"] }} ore.
  • Putting one together: use the valve, pick two Gas Tanks you carry (the list shows their pressure), and Assemble. The valve and both tanks become one Transfer Valve Bomb. Using the bomb opens the same panel, where Take Apart gives the valve and both tanks back, gas and all, as long as it isn't armed.
  • Examining a bomb reads both tanks' pressure. The Gas Analyzer reads what is in each side.
  • One side has to be hot. There is no spark: the fuel only lights if the gas, once mixed, is at its ignition temperature. Plasma lights at {{ g.GASES.Plasma.ignition }} K, so heat the Plasma well past that on the heater/freezer before you fill its tank, and put Oxygen in the other. Two cold tanks only mix.
  • More gas at the clean ratio of two parts fuel to three parts oxidizer makes a bigger blast. Nitrogen or Carbon Dioxide in the mix soaks up heat and makes it smaller.

Setting one off

  • Open Transfer Valve sets it off in your hands, after you confirm.
  • Set Timer: {{ b.TIMER_SECONDS|join(", ") }} seconds. Drop it and walk away; it goes off where it lies, or wherever you are if you still carry it. The floor shows the seconds left. Anyone can pick an armed bomb up and Disarm it before then. The {{ w.link("Research") }} teleporter can send one anywhere.
  • A bomb you set off on the station while no revealed antagonist is loose is an unauthorized detonation, a Dud included: a warrant goes out for you. With one loose it is no crime, wherever it goes off. See {{ w.link("Security and Jail") }}.
  • A Traitor may plant one. See {{ w.link("Antagonists") }}.

The Test Range

The Test Range at the Atmospherics Station sets a bomb you carry off safely and reads out its peak temperature, peak pressure and blast size. Nothing on the station is harmed, and it is never a crime. The bomb is used up: the valve and both tanks come back empty. The panel keeps the last {{ b.MAX_RANGE_LOG }} tests.

  • Central Command pays a bounty for the first test of each size in a shift: {% for size, credits in b.RANGE_BOUNTY.items() %}{{ size }} {{ credits }}{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} credits. A Dud pays nothing.
  • A Scientist posted here is sometimes asked for a test of one size. Running a bomb of that size on the range does the duty and pays the bounty's amount again.
