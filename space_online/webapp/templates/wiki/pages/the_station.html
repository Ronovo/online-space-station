Layout

The station is a hollow square ring of hallways. Your Quarters are at the hallway junction, and these rooms branch off the ring: {{ facts.rooms|join(", ") }}. The Station Map in your locker shows where each one is.

Getting around

  • In a hallway, the Go North, Go South, Go East and Go West buttons move you one tile. A room's door shows up as a button on the hallway tile in front of it.
  • A locked door keeps people out of a room, but never traps anyone inside. Whoever can work a room's station can lock and unlock its door.
  • If you've been drinking you may stumble and stay put, or fall and get hurt. See {{ w.link("Health") }}.

Inside a room

  • Everyone gets the room's options: what visitors can do there, like ordering a drink at the Bar.
  • If your job can work the room, you see its station menu first: the room's stations, Lock/Unlock Door and Room Options. Each job page lists the stations that job can use.
  • Exit Room puts you back in the hallway in front of the door.

Your Quarters

  • Bed: confirms your game is saved.
  • Storage Locker: store items and take them back out. See {{ w.link("Items and Hands") }}.
  • Computer: the {{ w.link("Stock Market") }}.
  • Door: back out to the hallway.

Hallway events

Each step along a hallway has a {{ (facts.events.HALLWAY_EVENT_CHANCE * 100)|round|int }}% chance of an event. It can be good (found credits, a supply crate, a market tip), neutral (announcements, station bots, a misbehaving vending machine), bad (slips, burns, falling debris) or a job event for your role. See {{ w.link("Errands and Job Events") }}. You'll also be told when crew pass you in the hall.

Lighting

Rooms and hallways follow the station's lighting power. Dimmed lights mean power is being saved, and a red glow means emergency lighting. See {{ w.link("Power") }}.
