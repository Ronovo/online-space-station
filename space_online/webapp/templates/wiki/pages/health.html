Limbs and damage

  • You have six limbs: head, chest, both arms and both legs. Each runs from 100% (unhurt) down to 0%. Above 75% is Healthy, above 40% is Injured, and anything lower is Critical.
  • On top of that you can take burn, poison and oxygen damage, each from 0% up to 100%. Under 10% is Clear, under 30% Mild, under 60% Moderate, and Severe after that.
  • Hallway accidents, falls while drunk, and failing life support are the usual causes.

Getting treated

  • In MedBay, Request Health Check is a free scan that shows what treatment would cost.
  • Talk to Doctor, while a Doctor is on duty, heals everything for a price per point of damage: {% for kind, multiplier in facts.cost_multipliers.items() %}{{ kind }} {{ multiplier }}{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} credits. See {{ w.link("Medical") }}.
  • A Medkit or an Aloe heals a little on the spot. See {{ w.link("Items and Hands") }}.

Oxygen

Oxygen damage comes from underpowered life support. While life support works, it wears off on its own below {{ facts.oxygen.DOCTOR_REQUIRED_FLOOR }}%. At {{ facts.oxygen.DOCTOR_REQUIRED_FLOOR }}% or more, only a Doctor can clear it. If it reaches {{ facts.oxygen.DEATH_THRESHOLD }}%, emergency systems revive you in your Quarters with every limb at {{ facts.oxygen.POST_DEATH_LIMB_HEALTH }}% and {{ facts.oxygen.POST_DEATH_OXYGEN }}% oxygen damage. See {{ w.link("Life Support") }}.

Alcohol

  • Alcoholic drinks raise your alcohol level, shown on the Character Sheet. It wears off by {{ facts.alcohol.ALCOHOL_DECAY_AMOUNT }}% every {{ facts.alcohol.ALCOHOL_DECAY_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes.
  • Above 5% you may stumble and fail to move. From 25% you may also fall and hurt a limb. The more you drink, the likelier both get.
  • At {{ facts.alcohol.DOCTOR_SOBER_FLOOR }}% or more, a Doctor can sober you up as part of treatment, for 1 credit per percent.
