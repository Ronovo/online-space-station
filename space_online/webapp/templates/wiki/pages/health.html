Limbs and damage

  • You have six limbs: head, chest, both arms and both legs. Each runs from 100% (unhurt) down to 0%. Only 100% is Healthy; above 75% is Minor injuries, above 40% is Injured, and anything lower is Critical. MedBay bills for every point below 100%.
  • On top of that you can take burn, poison and oxygen damage, each from 0% up to 100%. Under 10% is Clear, under 30% Mild, under 60% Moderate, and Severe after that.
  • Hallway accidents, falls while drunk, room hazards, fights and bad air are the usual causes.
  • Your overall health is the average of your limbs, brought down by burn, poison and oxygen damage. It shows in the status bar, and the screen flashes red whenever it drops.
  • A single limb at 0% does nothing on its own: only your overall health counts. A fight is lost when it falls below {{ facts.combat.DEFEAT_HEALTH }}%, which the fight screen shows next to your health.

Hunger and thirst

  • Both climb from 0% all shift: hunger by {{ facts.needs.NEED_PER_MINUTE.hunger }}% a minute and thirst by {{ facts.needs.NEED_PER_MINUTE.thirst }}%. The Character Sheet shows them, and you are told at {{ facts.needs.NEED_WARNING }}% and at 100%.
  • At 100%, a limb loses {{ facts.needs.STARVING_DAMAGE }}% every {{ facts.needs.STARVING_HURT_SECONDS }} seconds until you eat or drink.
  • Food brings hunger down: a cooked dish by {{ facts.feeds.dish }}%, a basic from the menu by {{ facts.feeds.basic }}%, and plain produce by {{ facts.needs.DEFAULT_FEEDS }}%. See {{ w.link("Kitchen") }}.
  • A drink brings thirst down by {{ facts.needs.DEFAULT_QUENCHES }}%, or less if it is alcoholic. See {{ w.link("Bar") }}.

Getting treated

  • In MedBay, Request Health Check is a free scan that shows what treatment would cost.
  • Talk to Doctor, while a Doctor is on duty, heals everything for a price per point of damage: {% for kind, multiplier in facts.cost_multipliers.items() %}{{ kind }} {{ multiplier }}{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} credits. See {{ w.link("Medical") }}.
  • A Medkit or an Aloe heals a little on the spot. See {{ w.link("Items and Hands") }}.
  • Rest in Bed in your Quarters mends every limb by {{ facts.rest_per_minute }}% a minute. The bed in the crew shuttle's Medical Bay mends them faster. See {{ w.link("Rounds and the Shuttle") }}.
  • Every treatment in MedBay uses a remedy from the station stores. With none left, nobody can be treated. See {{ w.link("Station Stores") }}.

Oxygen

Bad air gives oxygen or poison damage. Outside a Peaceful round, oxygen damage that reaches {{ facts.oxygen.DEATH_THRESHOLD }}% kills you. See {{ w.link("Life Support") }}.

Alcohol

  • Alcoholic drinks raise your alcohol level, shown on the Character Sheet. It wears off by {{ facts.alcohol.ALCOHOL_DECAY_AMOUNT }}% every {{ facts.alcohol.ALCOHOL_DECAY_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes.
  • Above 5% you may stumble and fail to move. From 25% you may also fall and hurt a limb. The more you drink, the likelier both get.
  • At {{ facts.alcohol.DOCTOR_SOBER_FLOOR }}% or more, a Doctor can sober you up as part of treatment, for 1 credit per percent.
