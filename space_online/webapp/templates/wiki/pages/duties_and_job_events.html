Duties

Most job events put a duty on your board. The Duties button is on every screen, shows how many are open, and opens what is waiting and how long each has left. Job events and duties go to the station log, not your PDA. You can have {{ d.MAX_OPEN_DUTIES }} at once, and no new one comes in while the board is full. A duty left for {{ d.DUTY_EXPIRY_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes is missed, except a Crop Request for a plant still growing. See {{ w.link("Botanist") }}.

  • Deliver: bring an item to a crew member in their room and press Deliver. The button shows while they're on duty.
  • Help: go to a crew member's room and press the Help button while they're on duty.
  • Serve: the Bartender serves a mixed drink from stock at the Bartender Station, and the Chef serves a dish from stock at the Chef Station.
  • Treat: the Doctor treats a hurt crew member from Crew Vitals.
  • Analyse: the Scientist files a correct analysis of an artifact.
  • Bomb Test: the Scientist runs a transfer valve bomb of the size asked for on the Test Range in Atmospherics.
  • Command Request: the Captain approves or denies it with the buttons on the Duties panel.

Engineers, Security Guards and the Head of Personnel have no board. Their work counts as a duty done when they repair a system or fix a hazard, make an arrest, or settle a request.

Rewards

  • Staff Assistant duties pay {{ e.ERRAND_MIN_REWARD }}-{{ e.ERRAND_MAX_REWARD }} credits.
  • Crop requests pay the Botanist {{ e.CROP_REQUEST_MIN_REWARD }}-{{ e.CROP_REQUEST_MAX_REWARD }} credits.
  • Drink and food orders pay the menu price.
  • Compound, ore, supply and gas requests pay {{ e.ITEM_REQUEST_MIN_REWARD }}-{{ e.ITEM_REQUEST_MAX_REWARD }} credits.
  • A bomb test pays the range bounty for its size: {% for size, credits in facts.bombs.RANGE_BOUNTY.items() %}{{ size }} {{ credits }}{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} credits.
  • An artifact analysis pays {{ facts.research.ANALYSIS_REWARD }} credits.
  • Requests at the service window are favours between the Bar and the Kitchen and pay nothing.
  • Patients pay the cost of their treatment, or what they have, if that is less.

The shift report says how many duties you did and how many you missed.
