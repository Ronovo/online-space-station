Anyone can trade shares from the Computer in their Quarters.

How prices move

  • The market lists {{ facts.company_count }} companies.
  • Every {{ facts.market_minutes }} minutes of game time a new market cycle starts, and each company's price moves up or down by as much as 10%.
  • Hallway events sometimes let you overhear a tip about a company. Tips aren't always right.

Trading

  • Select a company to see its price chart, then buy or sell whole shares at today's price.
  • Filter the list to companies you can afford, ones you own, or ones rising or falling, and sort it by price.
  • Your trade history lists every buy and sell. Your holdings are on the Character Sheet.
