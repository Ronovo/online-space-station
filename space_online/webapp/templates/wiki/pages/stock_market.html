Anyone can trade shares from the Computer in their Quarters. Your {{ w.link("PDA") }} shows the same market from anywhere, but it can't buy or sell.

How prices move

  • The market lists {{ facts.company_count }} companies.
  • Every {{ facts.market_minutes }} minutes of game time a new market cycle starts, and each company's price moves up or down at random by as much as 10%, unless real news pushes it (below).

Market news

  • Each cycle the market panel shows {{ facts.market.NEWS_PER_CYCLE }} headlines, each about one company and pointing up or down.
  • About {{ (facts.market.NEWS_REAL_CHANCE * 100)|round|int }} in 100 headlines are real. A real one moves its company that way at the next update, by {{ (facts.market.NEWS_MOVE_RANGE[0] * 100)|round|int }}-{{ (facts.market.NEWS_MOVE_RANGE[1] * 100)|round|int }}%. The rest are noise, and that company moves at random like any other.
  • Nothing tells you which headlines are real.
  • A hallway Market Tip event repeats one of the current headlines, so a tip is only as good as the news.

Trading

  • The company list shows each price, how it moved this cycle, and the shares you hold. The tiles above it show the cycle, the time to the next update, your credits and what your shares are worth.
  • Select a company to see its price chart, then buy or sell whole shares at today's price.
  • Filter the list to companies you can afford, ones you own, or ones rising or falling, and sort it by price.
  • Your trade history lists every buy and sell. Your holdings are on the Character Sheet.
  • When the shift ends, every share you still hold is sold at its current price, and the shift report shows what they raised.
