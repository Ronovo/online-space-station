What you do

Staff Assistants have no station of their own. You go where you're needed, run errands for the department crew, and use what every room offers visitors.

Job requests

Your job events are requests from department crew. Each one is an errand paying {{ e.ERRAND_MIN_REWARD }}-{{ e.ERRAND_MAX_REWARD }} credits, and you only get requests from crew who are aboard:
