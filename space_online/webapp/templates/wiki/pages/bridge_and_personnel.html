For visitors

Talk to Ship Leadership shows whoever leads the station:

  • Talk to Captain: the Captain is too busy for chats.
  • Talk to HoP, while the Head of Personnel is on duty:
    • Access Control: a public terminal where you request a change to another job.
    • Talk to HoP: the HoP looks at your pending request. It's a coin flip whether they approve it, and you change jobs on the spot if they do.

The Captain's Station

Only the Captain can work it.

  • Station Status: the full station briefing. See {{ w.link("Station Status") }}.
  • Security Alerts: who is wanted and still at large.
  • Crew Manifest: the crew by department.

The HoP's Station

The Head of Personnel and the Captain can work it.

  • Crew Manifest: the crew by department.
  • Job Assignments: give any crew member a new job, and they move to that job's post. Only the Captain can make someone Captain or move the current Captain, and the Captain is asked first before giving up the role with no one to take over.
  • Access Control: approve or deny the pending job request, whether it's yours or one a crew member sent in.

Giving someone the Engineer job switches the solar array on.

Requests

The Head of Personnel gets job requests from the crew, and the Captain gets command requests to approve or deny. See {{ w.link("Head of Personnel") }} and {{ w.link("Captain") }}.
