For visitors

Talk to Ship Leadership shows whoever leads the station:

  • Talk to Captain: the Captain is too busy for chats.
  • Talk to HoP, while the Head of Personnel is on duty:
    • Access Control: a public terminal where you request a change to another job. In a career it offers every job but Captain and Head of Personnel, unlocked or not: ending the shift in a job is how you unlock it. See {{ w.link("Modes and Careers") }}.
    • The HoP answers your request on your PDA {{ facts.bridge.ACCESS_DECISION_SECONDS_RANGE[0] }}-{{ facts.bridge.ACCESS_DECISION_SECONDS_RANGE[1] }} seconds after you put it in, or as soon as they can work after that. It's a coin flip whether they approve it, and you change jobs on the spot if they do. Until then Access Control shows the request as pending.

The Captain's Station

Only the Captain can work it.

  • Station Status: the full station briefing. See {{ w.link("Station Status") }}.
  • Security Alerts: who is wanted and still at large.
  • Incident Log: what the antagonists have done, and who was away from their post. See {{ w.link("Antagonists") }}.
  • Call Crew Shuttle: see {{ w.link("Rounds and the Shuttle") }}.
  • Crew Manifest: the crew by department.

The HoP's Station

The Head of Personnel and the Captain can work it.

  • Crew Manifest: the crew by department.
  • Job Assignments: give any crew member a new job, and they move to that job's post (the first one nobody holds, for a job with several), or take it up when they're let out of jail. Only the Captain can make someone Captain or move the current Captain. There is one Captain and one Head of Personnel at a time: giving either job to someone else asks first, then makes the one who held it a Staff Assistant. Taking the job away from the Head of Personnel, a Doctor, an Engineer or a Security Guard asks first too, and so does the Captain stepping down. Every assignment goes in the station log.
  • Access Control: approve or deny the pending job request, whether it's yours or one a crew member sent in.

Giving someone the Engineer job switches the solar array on.

Requests

The Head of Personnel gets job requests from the crew, and the Captain gets command requests to approve or deny. See {{ w.link("Head of Personnel") }} and {{ w.link("Captain") }}.
