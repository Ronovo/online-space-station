Growing plants

  1. Hold seeds in a hand, open View Plants at the station, pick a row, and use your hand on an empty planter.
  2. A plant grows through {{ b.GROWTH_STAGES }} stages, from Seedling to Mature. Each stage takes: {% for plant, minutes in facts.stage_minutes %}{{ plant }} {{ minutes }} minutes{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}.
  3. Water a plant with the Watering Can before each stage ends. A plant that reaches a new stage unwatered dies.
  4. When it's mature, Harvest it into your inventory.

Supplies

  • Miracle Grow: the rest of the plant's growth goes {{ (b.MIRACLE_GROW_SPEED_BONUS * 100)|round|int }}% faster.
  • Plentiful Harvest: the harvest yields {{ b.PLENTIFUL_HARVEST_YIELD_MULTIPLIER }} times as much.
  • "The Ooze" Mutagen: has no effect yet.
  • Trowel clears a dead or unwanted crop, and a Chainsaw clears a tree. Both are kept.
  • Supplies that would do nothing for a plant stay in your hand.

Keeping watch

You're told when a plant dies or is ready to harvest. Station Status counts planters that need water or have dead plants. See {{ w.link("Station Status") }}.
