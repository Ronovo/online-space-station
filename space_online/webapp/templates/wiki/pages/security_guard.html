What you do

You keep order. At the Security Station you see the crew manifest, issue warrants and fines, and run the jail: pardoning prisoners or adding time to their sentences. You can also lock and unlock the Security door. See {{ w.link("Security and Jail") }}.

Arrests

  • When you walk into a room, you scan it for warrants and collect any unpaid fines from the crew inside.
  • When you meet wanted crew in a room or the halls, or call one back to their post, you choose to Arrest or Let Go. Some offer a bribe to be let go.
  • You collect unpaid fines from crew you pass in the halls. Anyone who can't or won't pay goes to jail.

Job events

Witness a Crime: if a crew member is in the hall with you, a warrant goes out for them and you can arrest them. Otherwise you don't catch who did it.
