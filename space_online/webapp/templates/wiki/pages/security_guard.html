What you do

You keep order. At the Security Station you see the crew manifest, issue warrants and fines, read the incident log, and run the jail: pardoning prisoners or adding time to their sentences. You can also lock and unlock the Security Office door. See {{ w.link("Security and Jail") }}.

Arrests

  • You scan rooms for warrants, collect fines, and arrest or let go the wanted crew you meet. See {{ w.link("Security and Jail") }}.
  • Your PDA's Scan Crew reads the record of anyone standing where you are, and how many of the shift's incidents they were away from their post for. It also messages you about warrants and antagonists. See {{ w.link("PDA") }}.

Antagonists

  • In a fight, a jab from the Stun Baton makes the enemy lose its next turns, and Handcuffs on a stunned antagonist end the fight at once. See {{ w.link("Combat and Weapons") }}.
  • NPC Security Guards hunt revealed antagonists on their own. See {{ w.link("Antagonists") }}.
  • A warrant raised on an anonymous tip has nobody standing behind it. You can clear it from Issue Warrant/Fine.
  • The Incident Log lists who was away from their post for each incident. Whoever is away every time is your suspect: a warrant and an arrest ends a hidden antagonist before it strikes.
  • While the shuttle is docked you can watch for a hidden antagonist, and you can arrest wanted crew into its cell. See {{ w.link("Rounds and the Shuttle") }}.

Job events

Witness a Crime: it only comes while you are out in the hallways. A crew member who could be up to no good, anywhere aboard and not already wanted, is named, and a warrant goes out for them that you can arrest them on. With nobody to name, you don't catch who did it.
