The Research Lab is the south door of the Science Hall, across from {{ w.link("Atmospherics") }} and beside {{ w.link("Chemistry") }}. It has two stations behind one door: the Artifact Section, with a bench for working out alien artifacts, and the Teleporter Room. Scientists, the Head of Personnel and the Captain can work both. See {{ w.link("Scientist") }}.

For visitors

  • Leave an Artifact on the Bench, if you carry one nobody has worked out: Central Command pays a finder's fee (not to a Scientist) of {{ r.FINDERS_FEE }} credits. The bench holds {{ r.MAX_BENCH }}.
  • Talk to Scientist, while the Scientist posted here is on duty.

Where artifacts come from

  • A Miner searching a planet now and then digs one up, and it comes home with the ore. See {{ w.link("Mining") }}.
  • Central Command sends them up to the Scientist posted here to be worked out. See {{ w.link("Scientist") }}.

The Artifact Bench

In the Artifact Section. Every artifact is set off by one of the four test rigs, at or above a strength only it knows: {% for name in a.DEVICES.values() %}{{ name }}{{ ", " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}.

  1. Pick an artifact, a rig and a strength from 1 to {{ a.MAX_STRENGTH }}, and press Run Test.
  2. The wrong rig does nothing. The right rig at too low a strength gives a faint hum, and a shudder once you are within {{ a.CLOSE_MARGIN }} of what it needs.
  3. At the right strength the artifact comes alive, and what it does then tells you what it is.
  4. More than {{ a.OVERLOAD_MARGIN }} over what it needs overloads it, which throws you against the wall and costs a limb {{ a.OVERLOAD_DAMAGE }}%. Work up from a low strength.
  5. File Analysis with what it is and what sets it off. If both are right, Central Command pays {{ r.ANALYSIS_REWARD }} credits and the artifact is yours. If not, the form comes back saying which part is wrong, and you can file again.

The Teleporter

In the Teleporter Room. The teleporter sends you, or an item, to any tile of the station, locked doors or not. Its console does not take station coordinates. It takes two numbers, X and Y, and where they come out is different every shift.

The formula

On each axis, the console number for a place is

console = m × station + c

  • station is the place's coordinate: North for X, East for Y. Every hallway screen shows its own, and the teleporter panel lists every room's.
  • m is a multiplier: {{ a.TELE_MULTIPLIERS|join(", ") }}.
  • c is an offset, from {{ a.TELE_OFFSET_RANGE[0] }} to {{ a.TELE_OFFSET_RANGE[1] }}.
  • X and Y each have their own m and c, picked afresh every shift.

Working it out

Send Beacon sends a test beacon to the numbers you typed. It reports the station coordinates it came out at, even out in space, and comes straight back. Two sends are enough for both axes:

  1. Send the beacon to console numbers a1, and note the coordinate it reports, r1.
  2. Send it again to a different number a2, and note r2.
  3. m = (a2 - a1) ÷ (r2 - r1)
  4. c = a1 - m × r1

Do that once with the X numbers and the North readings, and once with the Y numbers and the East readings.

A worked example

You send the beacon to X 10, Y 10 and it reports 3.5 North, 2.75 East. You send it to X 20, Y 30 and it reports 8.5 North, 7.75 East.

  • X: m = (20 - 10) ÷ (8.5 - 3.5) = 2, and c = 10 - 2 × 3.5 = 3.
  • Y: m = (30 - 10) ÷ (7.75 - 2.75) = 4, and c = 10 - 4 × 2.75 = -1.
  • To reach the Security Office at 6 North, 6 East: X = 2 × 6 + 3 = 15 and Y = 4 × 6 - 1 = 23.

The console

  • Scan: says whether there is floor at those numbers. Numbers that come out between tiles, or off the station, have none.
  • Send Beacon: reports where the numbers come out, as above.
  • Send Me: puts you there. It only fires at a tile with floor.
  • Send Item: sends one of the item you chose. It lies on the floor there until someone picks it up.
  • Receive: brings back everything lying on the floor there.
