The Cargo Bay is off the Northeast Hallway, next to the Mining Bay. It is where the station buys from Central Command and sells to it.

For visitors

Both counters are open while the Quartermaster is on duty.

  • Buy Supplies: the supply counter sells supplies on the spot, at {{ c.COUNTER_MARKUP }} times what the Quartermaster pays for them.
  • Order Console: request something from Central Command. It is open whether or not the Quartermaster is in, and your PDA has one too. See below.
  • Collect Your Order: takes what you requested off the conveyor once its pod has docked.
  • Export Counter: a Sell button for each kind of thing you carry that the counter takes, and Sell All for everything at once, duty items included. This is where a Miner sells ore, and the ore goes up to the station stores. See {{ w.link("Station Stores") }}.

The Order Console

Everyone but the Quartermaster can use it, from the Cargo Bay's room options or from the PDA, so a request can go in from anywhere on the station. See {{ w.link("PDA") }}.

  • Pick a supply or a canister of gas and press Request. It costs the crew price in the table below, the same as over the counter. Nothing is charged yet.
  • The Quartermaster answers {{ c.REQUEST_DECISION_SECONDS_RANGE[0] }}-{{ c.REQUEST_DECISION_SECONDS_RANGE[1] }} seconds later, in the station log and on your PDA. If you can pay, they approve it, charge you and order it, and it docks {{ c.CARGO_DELIVERY_SECONDS }} seconds after that. If you can't, they deny it and charge nothing.
  • A Quartermaster who is in jail, too hurt to work, away from the bay or cleared out by a hazard answers nothing until they are back. The console says why your request is waiting.
  • The console lists your requests: waiting on the Quartermaster, on its way with the time left, ready to collect, or denied with the reason.
  • What you requested waits at the Cargo Bay for you. Press Collect Your Order there.
  • A canister of gas goes on to Atmospherics instead. See Dangerous gas below.

The Cargo Station

Quartermasters, the Head of Personnel and the Captain can work it.

  • Order Supplies: the Quartermaster's own Order Console. Each order costs the Quartermaster's price and comes up in a supply pod {{ c.CARGO_DELIVERY_SECONDS }} seconds later.
  • Supply Requests, on the same console: the departments' requests on your Duties board. Approve orders the supply, and you still collect it and deliver it for the reward. Deny turns it down, which counts as a duty done. See {{ w.link("Quartermaster") }}.
  • Collect Deliveries: takes everything you ordered that has docked off the conveyor.
  • Export Counter: the same counter visitors use, open to you whether or not anyone else is in.

Canisters of gas are only sold through the Order Console, not over the counter.

Dangerous gas

  • A full canister of {{ facts.gases.DANGEROUS_GASES|join(", ") }} can be ordered on either Order Console. When its pod docks it goes down the tube into an empty slot on the Atmospherics Canister Rack. See {{ w.link("Atmospherics") }}.
  • With no empty slot on the rack, the canister waits at the Cargo Bay, and the console says to clear a slot. It goes down the tube as soon as one is free.
  • Every order of dangerous gas goes in the Incident Log, with who ordered it and when. The Quartermaster never refuses one for being dangerous, but your first {{ c.DANGEROUS_ORDERS_ALLOWED }} in a shift are all you get for free: each one after that has a {{ (c.ORDER_ALERT_CHANCE * 100)|round|int }}% chance of putting out a warrant for you, for stockpiling dangerous gas. See {{ w.link("Security and Jail") }}.
