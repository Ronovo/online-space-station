Inventory

Open your inventory from the Character Sheet. Items of the same kind stack. Select an item to Examine it, or open its Actions:

  • Read books like the Welcome Guide and the Station Map.
  • Eat food and Drink drinks, which bring your hunger and thirst down. Alcoholic drinks raise your alcohol level. See {{ w.link("Health") }}.
  • Use a Medkit, Aloe or a remedy to heal, an analysed artifact, the Gas Analyzer to read the air and your tanks, or a Tank Transfer Valve or bomb to work its valve. Use also opens a Basic Tools set. A Gas Tank has Open Valve and Close Valve. See {{ w.link("Atmospherics") }}.
  • Equip an item into one of your hands.
  • Drop an item. It stays on the floor where you dropped it, in a room or a hallway, and shows under the screen there with a Pick Up button.

Hands

You have a left and a right hand, and the Hands button is on every screen. Some things only work from your hands: seeds, liquids and tools are used on Botany planters by holding them. See {{ w.link("Botany") }}. Your PDA only opens and scans while it is in a hand. See {{ w.link("PDA") }}. You breathe from a Gas Tank only while it is in a hand with its valve open. Unequipping puts the item back in your inventory.

Your storage locker

The locker in your Quarters starts with: {{ facts.locker_items|join(", ") }}. Take items out to carry them, and store anything you don't need right now.

Useful items

  • Medkit: heals every injured limb by 10% and lowers each kind of damage by 10%. It's kept if you're already at full health.
  • Aloe: lowers burn damage by {{ facts.aloe_relief }}%. It's kept if you have no burns.
  • Basic Tools: a set holding a Wrench, Screwdriver and Wirecutters. Use it to pull a tool out or put one back. Carrying the set lets you repair faulted systems. See {{ w.link("Engineering") }}.
  • Wrench, Wirecutters and the Fire Extinguisher fix room hazards. See {{ w.link("Hazards") }}. A tool still inside a Basic Tools set you carry counts.
  • Screwdriver and Wirecutters together force a locked door. See {{ w.link("The Station") }}.

Where items come from

  • The Engineering fabricator makes tools, books and weapons for anyone, out of the ore in the station stores. See {{ w.link("Engineering") }}.
  • The {{ w.link("Cargo") }} Bay's supply counter sells supplies, and the Order Console, there or on your PDA, takes requests.
  • The Tank Rack in {{ w.link("Atmospherics") }} hands out a Gas Tank and a Gas Analyzer, and one Tank Transfer Valve a shift.
  • The Botany vending machine and the Botanist's produce stand sell seeds, supplies and produce. See {{ w.link("Botany") }}.
  • The Bar sells drinks and the Kitchen sells food. See {{ w.link("Bar") }} and {{ w.link("Kitchen") }}.
  • Hallway supply crates and vending machines sometimes hand out items.
