Inventory

Open your inventory from the Character Sheet. Items of the same kind stack. Select an item to Examine it, or open its Actions:

  • Read books like the Welcome Guide and the Station Map.
  • Eat food and Drink drinks. Alcoholic drinks raise your alcohol level.
  • Use a Medkit or Aloe to heal, or open a Basic Tools set.
  • Equip an item into one of your hands.
  • Drop an item. It asks first, because a dropped item is gone for good.

Hands

You have a left and a right hand, and the Hands button is on every screen. Some things only work from your hands: seeds, liquids and tools are used on Botany planters by holding them. See {{ w.link("Botany") }}. Unequipping puts the item back in your inventory.

Your storage locker

The locker in your Quarters starts with: {{ facts.locker_items|join(", ") }}. Take items out to carry them, and store anything you don't need right now.

Useful items

  • Medkit: heals every injured limb by 10% and lowers each kind of damage by 10%. It's kept if you're already at full health.
  • Aloe: lowers burn damage by {{ facts.aloe_relief }}%. It's kept if you have no burns.
  • Basic Tools: a set holding a Wrench, Screwdriver and Wirecutters. Use it to pull a tool out or put one back. Carrying the set lets you repair faulted systems. See {{ w.link("Engineering") }}.

Where items come from

  • The Engineering fabricator makes tools and books for anyone. See {{ w.link("Engineering") }}.
  • The Botany vending machine and the Botanist's produce stand sell seeds, supplies and produce. See {{ w.link("Botany") }}.
  • The Bar sells drinks. See {{ w.link("Bar") }}.
  • Hallway supply crates and vending machines sometimes hand out items.
