Fights are turn-based, and once one starts you can't walk away from it. You fight the monsters on a {{ w.link("Mining") }} trip and the station's {{ w.link("Antagonists") }}.

Your turn

  • Attack: hits for the attack of the stronger item in your hands. Bare hands hit for {{ k.UNARMED_ATTACK }}, and something that isn't a weapon for {{ k.IMPROVISED_ATTACK }}. A second weapon adds nothing, so the other hand is better spent on something that blocks.
  • Block: you don't attack, but this turn's hit is cut by a further {{ k.BRACE_BLOCK }}%, and your next Attack is a counter-attack for {{ k.COUNTER_MULTIPLIER }} times the damage.
  • Stun, with a Stun Baton in hand: the enemy loses its next turns. The baton then takes {{ k.STUN_RECHARGE_TURNS }} turns to recharge.
  • Cuff, with Handcuffs on you: arrests a stunned enemy and ends the fight at once. It only works on antagonists that can be handcuffed.
  • Hands: change what you are holding. It doesn't cost a turn. See {{ w.link("Items and Hands") }}.

The enemy's turn

  • It hits one of your limbs. Some enemies also burn or poison. See {{ w.link("Health") }}.
  • Aboard the crew shuttle in flight it doesn't wait for you: if you don't act, it strikes every {{ k.TRANSIT_ENEMY_TURN_SECONDS }} seconds. See {{ w.link("Rounds and the Shuttle") }}.
  • Anything in your hands with a block value stops that share of every hit, up to {{ k.MAX_BLOCK }}%.
  • If your overall health falls below {{ k.DEFEAT_HEALTH }}%, you have lost. Against a monster the rescue drone flies you home. Against an antagonist you die.
