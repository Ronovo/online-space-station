The Engineering Station

Engineers, the Head of Personnel and the Captain can work it. Its Engineering Panel runs the station's power: battery status, the solar array switch, a level for each system, and the power modes. See {{ w.link("Power") }}.

System faults

  • Equipment faults can knock out {{ facts.faultable|join(", ") }}. A faulted system shows FAULT on the panel and in Station Status, and stays at 0 until it's repaired. Power modes skip it, and its level won't go back up.
  • To repair it, carry or hold Basic Tools or a Repair Tool and press Repair next to it on the panel. It comes back at the current power mode's level.
  • Basic Tools start in your locker, and both can be made at the fabricator.

Solar outages

As an Engineer, a hallway job event can shut the solar array down. Switch it back on from the Engineering Panel. See {{ w.link("Engineer") }}.
