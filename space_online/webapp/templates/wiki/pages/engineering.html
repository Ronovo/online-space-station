The Engineering Station

Engineers, the Head of Personnel and the Captain can work it. Its Engineering Panel runs the station's power: battery status, what the solar array and the Combustion Engine are charging, the solar array switch, a level for each system, and the power modes. See {{ w.link("Power") }}. Its Combustion Engine panel runs the engine below.

The Combustion Engine

The station's main power. It burns a fuel and an oxidizer in a burn chamber of {{ e.BURN_CHAMBER.volume }} L, rated to {{ e.BURN_CHAMBER.rated }} kPa. The chamber gas heats the hot loop, a radiator cools the cold loop toward the cold of space, and the generator between the two charges the battery in step with how far apart they are, up to {{ e.OUTPUT_CAP|int }}% a minute. The spent gas goes out to space. It uses the same gas rules as {{ w.link("Atmospherics") }}.

The canister bay

  • The bay has {{ e.BAY_SLOTS }} slots. A shift starts with a canister of {% for name, role, gas in e.LINES.values() %}{{ gas }} on the {{ name }} line{{ " and one of " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}, enough to run at the safe setting for the longest shift: the Oxygen lasts about {{ (e.CANISTER_MOLES / (e.SAFE_SETTINGS.injection * e.INJECTION_STEP * (1 - e.SAFE_SETTINGS.mixer / 100)) / 60)|round|int }} minutes.
  • Canisters come down the tube from Atmospherics, sent with Send to Engineering at the Canister Rack, and go back with Send to Atmospherics. The other end needs a free slot, and a connected canister has to be disconnected first.
  • A canister in the bay does nothing until you Connect as Fuel or Connect as Oxidizer. One canister fits on a line; connecting another takes the first one off. A pre-mixed canister on the Fuel line with the mixer at 100 works too.
  • While the engine runs you can't take a canister off a line it is drawing from. Shut it down first.

The controls

  • Mixer ratio: the share of the injected gas that comes from the Fuel line, 0 to 100. The clean burn is two parts fuel to three parts oxidizer. Too lean runs cool, and too rich sends unburnt fuel out of the exhaust.
  • Injection rate: 0 to 10, each step {{ e.INJECTION_STEP|round(2) }} mol a second. More gas burns hotter and gives more, until the hot loop reaches its limit or the chamber its rating.
  • Coolant flow: 0 to 10, how hard the radiator cools the cold loop. More flow widens the gap between the loops and raises the output. With none the two loops meet, the output falls to nothing, and the hot loop keeps climbing.
  • Ignite lights it, given a canister on the Fuel line. Shut Down stops the injection. Emergency Vent dumps the chamber to space and stops the engine.

Calibrating is finding, for the gases you have connected, the mixer ratio that burns clean and the injection and coolant that hold the hot loop just under its limit. Nothing tells you the numbers: the gauges and the hint line do.

When it goes wrong

  • Pumps stall: over {{ e.BURN_CHAMBER.rated }} kPa, injection and exhaust stop until the pressure drops, and the output sags. The log warns at {{ (e.STALL_WARNING * 100)|int }}%.
  • Overheat: while the engine runs with the hot loop over {{ e.HOT_LIMIT }} K, the chamber takes {{ e.OVERHEAT_DAMAGE|int }}% damage a second. The log says when the loop goes over, and the log and an Engineer's PDA warn at {% for level in e.DAMAGE_WARNINGS %}{{ level }}%{{ " and " if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} damage.
  • Chamber fire: at 100% the engine shuts down and a fire breaks out in the Engineering Bay. The engine stays dead until it is repaired.
  • Burst: if the chamber is past {{ e.BURST_PRESSURE }} times its rating when it fails, it bursts instead: a hull breach in the Engineering Bay, and the blast takes {{ facts.atmos.RUPTURE_DAMAGE }}% off a limb if you are in the room. It never kills outright.
  • Flame-out: with nothing left to burn, from an empty canister or the injection at 0, the engine goes out. The log says so. It does no damage.
  • Repair Chamber, with the engine shut down and Basic Tools or a Repair Tool on you, clears the chamber's damage, the same way a faulted system is repaired.
  • Cut coolant line: sabotage can cut it. Repair Coolant Line with the same tools. See {{ w.link("Antagonists") }}.

The NPC Engineer

When the Engineer is a crew member the engine is already running when the shift starts. On a turn with no hazard, fault, solar outage or dead life support to see to, they repair a cut coolant line, shut down an overheating engine, or light a stopped one at the safe setting once it has gas on both lines, connecting a spare canister from the bay to a line that has run low. Next they bring hallway lighting an antagonist dimmed back to where it was and report the tampering to Security. With nothing else to do, they send a nearly empty canister that isn't connected back down the tube to Atmospherics for refilling, once the rack there has a free slot. When you are the Engineer, nobody does any of it for you.

  • A Head of Personnel shift has no NPC Engineer, so the engine and the solar array both start off.
  • The NPC Engineer never repairs a wrecked burn chamber.

System faults

  • Equipment faults can knock out {{ facts.faultable|join(", ") }}. A System Fault job event never picks one that is switched off. A faulted system shows FAULT on the panel and in Station Status, and stays at 0 until it's repaired. Power modes skip it, and its level won't go back up.
  • To repair it, carry or hold Basic Tools or a Repair Tool and press Repair next to it on the panel. It comes back at the level it had before the fault.
  • Basic Tools start in your locker, and both can be made at the fabricator.

Solar outages and hazards

As an Engineer, your job can shut the solar array down or fault a system. Switch the array back on from the Engineering Panel. Hazards are yours too: deal with each one in its room. See {{ w.link("Engineer") }} and {{ w.link("Hazards") }}. When the Engineer is a crew member, they see to all of it themselves.
