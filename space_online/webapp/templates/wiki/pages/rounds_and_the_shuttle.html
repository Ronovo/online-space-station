A round is one shift on the station. It ends when the crew shuttle carries the crew back to Central Command, which takes {{ (r.SHUTTLE_LOCKOUT_SECONDS + r.SHUTTLE_URGENT_ARRIVAL_SECONDS + trip) // 60 }} to {{ (r.SHIFT_END_RANGE_SECONDS[1] + r.SHUTTLE_ARRIVAL_SECONDS + trip) // 60 }} minutes of play, or longer after a recall. The clock only runs while the game is open, so a shift waits for you. The bar above every screen shows the alert level, the shift clock, your health, and the shuttle once it has been called.

Who calls the shuttle

  • Nobody can call it in the first {{ r.SHUTTLE_LOCKOUT_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes of a shift.
  • When you aren't the Captain, the Captain crew member calls it at the first of these:
    • the alert reaches Red;
    • {{ r.ALL_CLEAR_DELAY_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes after the last threat is dealt with, but never before {{ r.SHIFT_END_RANGE_SECONDS[0] // 60 }} minutes in;
    • the shift has simply run its course, some time between {{ r.SHIFT_END_RANGE_SECONDS[0] // 60 }} and {{ r.SHIFT_END_RANGE_SECONDS[1] // 60 }} minutes in.
    If no Captain can work, nobody calls it at Red or when the threats are dealt with. Central Command calls it only when the shift has run its course.
  • If you are the Captain, when to call it is up to you, Red alert included: Call Crew Shuttle is at the Captain's Station on the Bridge. Central Command only calls it if the shift runs long, at {{ r.SHIFT_END_RANGE_SECONDS[1] // 60 }} minutes.

  • Recall: a Captain can recall the shuttle until it is halfway to the station, at most {{ r.SHUTTLE_RECALLS_PER_SHIFT }} recall per shift. After a recall the shuttle can't be called again for as long as that trip would have taken. A call that comes due in that time goes out the moment it ends, and the log says when that will be.
  • Boarding: Departures is off the hallway just north of the junction by your Quarters. While the shuttle is docked, press Board the Shuttle there. You board into the Common Area, on your feet, and can step back off from there until it leaves.
  • Left behind: if you aren't aboard when it leaves, you don't survive the round, in a Peaceful round too. If you are in jail, Security puts you aboard in cuffs and locks you in the shuttle's cell.
  • The crew: crew who aren't at a post head for Departures as soon as the shuttle is called. Crew at their posts keep working until it docks, then go. About one in {{ (1 / facts.npcs.STAY_BEHIND_CHANCE)|round|int }} doesn't notice and stays behind. Jailed crew are put aboard, and crew too hurt to move are left. A recall sends everyone back to what they were doing. The log says how many made it when the shuttle leaves.
  • In flight: a fight with an antagonist aboard goes on every {{ facts.combat.TRANSIT_ENEMY_TURN_SECONDS }} seconds whether you act or not, and no salary is paid once the shuttle has left. See {{ w.link("Antagonists") }} for who it goes for.

Aboard the shuttle

The shuttle has five sections. You walk between them through the Common Area, and Exit Room takes you back to it. The crew who board sit in the Common Area's seats.

  • Strapping in: the shuttle warns {{ r.LAUNCH_WARNING_SECONDS }} seconds before it leaves. Be strapped into a seat, lying in the bed or locked in the cell when it does: anyone else is thrown across the cabin and hurts a limb by {{ r.LAUNCH_THROW_DAMAGE_RANGE[0] }}-{{ r.LAUNCH_THROW_DAMAGE_RANGE[1] }}%, in a Peaceful round too, though it can't kill you. While strapped in you can still talk to the crew, but you have to unstrap to get up. Once the shuttle is away you can unstrap and walk the sections again.
  • Talk to the Crew: each crew member in the seats says one thing about the shift: an antagonist still loose or dealt with, the Incident Log, a hazard left burning, the battery or life support, someone who didn't make the shuttle, your warrant, or your duties. Each comes up once a trip. With nothing left to say about the shift, they talk about their job. The log names who said it and their job. Prisoners in the cell say nothing.
  • Head Count: every crew member and where they are: aboard, on the way, hasn't noticed the call, in jail or the shuttle's cell, or too hurt to move. While docked, Page sends someone who hasn't noticed the call to the shuttle. It needs the Communication Array, pays nothing, and they still have to walk there, so a late page may not get them aboard. In flight it shows who was left behind.
  • Watch the Passengers (docked only) shows what one crew member in the seats is up to. A hidden antagonist aboard sometimes gives itself away, but innocent crew can look odd too, so one look proves nothing. Issue a Warrant puts a warrant on a crew member in the seats for suspicious behaviour aboard the shuttle. A wrong warrant costs you nothing, but the innocent crew member rides in the cell.
  • The cell: Scan the Passengers in the Security section collects the fines of the crew aboard and offers to arrest the wanted, as walking into a room does on the station. They go into the shuttle's cell, and the cell lists who is held and what for. If a hidden antagonist is secretly one of the crew, arresting that crew member ends it before it strikes. Nobody is let out of the cell, and the station's prisoners join it when the shuttle leaves. A prisoner rides in the cell for the whole flight and still reaches Central Command.
  • The bed in the Medical Bay works like the bed in your Quarters, faster: it mends every limb by {{ facts.shuttle_bed_per_minute }}% a minute, for free. It doesn't treat burns, poison or oxygen damage. Get up before you leave the section. A fight aboard gets you out of it.
  • Coffee: put Coffee Grounds and Water in the Break Room's coffee machine and press Brew for a Coffee, which quenches thirst like any other drink. Water alone runs through clear and grounds alone scorch.
  • The flight: one thing happens {{ r.FLIGHT_EVENT_RANGE_SECONDS[0] }} to {{ r.FLIGHT_EVENT_RANGE_SECONDS[1] }} seconds into the flight, unless an antagonist aboard starts a fight:
    • Turbulence hurts a limb by {{ facts.events.TURBULENCE_DAMAGE_RANGE[0] }}-{{ facts.events.TURBULENCE_DAMAGE_RANGE[1] }}% if you aren't strapped in, in the bed or in the cell, and spills any Coffee you carry.
    • Taken Ill: a crew member in the seats needs a remedy from Chemistry. If you carry the right one you can give it to them, and they pay you {{ facts.events.FLIGHT_REMEDY_REWARD_RANGE[0] }}-{{ facts.events.FLIGHT_REMEDY_REWARD_RANGE[1] }} credits.
    • Loose Cargo: grab the crate for whatever is in it, at the risk of a knock, or let it slide.
    • Central Command asks how the shift went. Your answer only changes what goes in the log.

The shift report

When the shuttle reaches Central Command, or when you die, you get a shift report: how the round ended, who the antagonists were, what they were after and what became of them, the duties you did and missed, your pay, and in a career what you unlocked or a pick of what to unlock. See {{ w.link("Modes and Careers") }}.
