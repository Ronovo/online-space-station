For visitors

  • Order Drinks, while the Bartender is on duty: the drink menu has basic drinks plus any mixed drinks the bar has in stock. Filter it by basic, mixed, alcoholic or non-alcoholic. A drink goes into your inventory to drink whenever you like.
  • Socialize: chat with whoever's around.
  • Alcoholic drinks raise your alcohol level. See {{ w.link("Health") }}.

The Bartender Station

Bartenders, the Head of Personnel and the Captain can work it.

  • Mix Drinks: pour up to {{ facts.max_pours }} ingredients into a glass and mix. If they match a recipe, the drink goes into the bar's stock and onto the menu. If not, you're told what went wrong.
  • View Recipes: the recipe book, by category, with a search by name, ingredient or description, and the full list of ingredients.
  • Serve Regular Drinks: the drink menu. Drinks are free for the Bartender.

Drink orders

As a Bartender, a hallway job event can bring a crew member's order for a mixed drink. Mix it, then serve it from the Bartender Station for the drink's menu price. See {{ w.link("Bartender") }}.
