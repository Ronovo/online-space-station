Errands

Some job events give you an errand. You have one at a time, and no new errand comes in until it's done. A pending errand shows in Station Status.

  • Deliver: bring an item to a crew member in their room and press Deliver. The button shows while they're on duty.
  • Help out: go to a crew member's room and press Help Out while they're on duty.
  • Serve: the Bartender serves a mixed drink from stock at the Bartender Station.
  • Treat: the Doctor treats a hurt crew member from Crew Vitals.

Rewards

  • Staff Assistant errands pay {{ e.ERRAND_MIN_REWARD }}-{{ e.ERRAND_MAX_REWARD }} credits.
  • Crop requests pay the Botanist {{ e.CROP_REQUEST_MIN_REWARD }}-{{ e.CROP_REQUEST_MAX_REWARD }} credits.
  • Drink orders pay the drink's menu price.
  • Patients pay the cost of their treatment.
