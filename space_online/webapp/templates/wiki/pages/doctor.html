What you do

You keep the crew healthy. At the MedBay Station, Heal Injuries fully heals you for free, and Check Crew Vitals shows everyone's health. Injured crew can be treated from there, and they pay you the cost of their treatment. You can also lock and unlock the MedBay door. See {{ w.link("Medical") }}.

Job events

Patient: a crew member is hurt in an accident. Treat them from Crew Vitals to finish the errand. See {{ w.link("Errands and Job Events") }}.

Tips

  • Crew pick up small injuries as they wander. Check the vitals monitor now and then for paying patients.
  • When life support fails, everyone takes oxygen damage. Past {{ facts.oxygen.DOCTOR_REQUIRED_FLOOR }}% it won't wear off on its own. See {{ w.link("Life Support") }}.
