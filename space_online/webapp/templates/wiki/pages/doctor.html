What you do

You keep the crew healthy. At the MedBay Station, Heal Injuries fully heals you for no credits, using one of MedBay's remedies, and can't be used while MedBay has none. Check Crew Vitals shows everyone's health. Injured crew can be treated from there, and they pay you the cost of their treatment, or what they have, if that is less. Every treatment uses a remedy from MedBay's stock. You can also lock and unlock the MedBay door. See {{ w.link("Medical") }}.

Job events

Patient: a crew member is hurt in an accident. Treat them from Crew Vitals to finish the duty. See {{ w.link("Duties and Job Events") }}.

Tips

  • A crew member in Critical condition stops working, and with you in the job nobody else treats them. See {{ w.link("Crew") }}.
  • Watch the remedy count. If it runs low, ask after the Scientist in Chemistry. See {{ w.link("Station Stores") }}.
  • Your PDA shows the Crew Vitals Monitor from anywhere, and messages you when someone turns critical. Treating them still takes MedBay. See {{ w.link("PDA") }}.
  • When life support fails, a hull breach vents the air, or the air is poisoned, everyone breathing it takes damage. Past {{ facts.oxygen.DOCTOR_REQUIRED_FLOOR }}% oxygen damage it won't wear off on its own. See {{ w.link("Life Support") }}.
