Everyone starts a shift with a PDA in their inventory. It is an item like any other: you can drop it, leave it in your locker, or lose it. It only works while it is in one of your hands. See {{ w.link("Items and Hands") }}.

Opening it

Equip the PDA to a hand and a PDA button appears with Character Sheet, Duties and Hands. The button shows how many messages you haven't read. Take the PDA out of your hand and the button goes away.

Order ConsoleRequest a supply or a canister of gas from Central Command, from anywhere. The Quartermaster answers on your PDA, and you collect it at the Cargo Bay. Every job but the Quartermaster has it. A canister of gas goes to Atmospherics instead. See {{ w.link("Cargo") }}.
StocksThe {{ w.link("Stock Market") }}, to look at. Buying and selling still take the computer in your quarters.
FilesThe files on your computer, to read.
Your job's appSome jobs have one more entry. See below.

Messages

  • Messages are for what needs your attention. Duties aren't sent here: they are on the Duties panel and in the station log. See {{ w.link("Duties and Job Events") }}.
  • An event that hurts you, or leaves something on the floor where you are, is sent here as well as to the station log.
  • Replies come here, such as the Head of Personnel answering your job request or the Quartermaster answering an order.
  • Some jobs are also told when something they look after needs them. You get one message when the need starts, and another if it comes back after you dealt with it.

Messages reach the PDA whether you are holding it or not. You read them the next time you open it.

Scanning

To scan something related to your job, use the PDA on the object. What it reads depends on your job, and the reading goes to the station log. In a job with no scanner for the thing, it says so.

JobUse the PDA onIt reads
BotanistA planter, with the PDA's button under it at the Botany Station The plant, its stage, the time to the next one, what has been applied, the yield and the chance of a mutant harvest
ChefAn item in your inventoryWhether it can be eaten, how much hunger it feeds and, for mutant produce, what it mends
BartenderAn item in your inventoryWhat a mixed drink is mixed from. A plain drink reads as itself
ScientistAn item in your inventoryWhat it goes into the beaker as, what the processor makes of it, what a compound is made of and what it treats, or a Gas Tank's gauge
QuartermasterAn item in your inventoryIts order price, its counter price and what the export counter pays
MinerA vein you don't recognise, on a planetThe ore, what it exports for and where it is found. See {{ w.link("Mining") }}

To scan an item, open your inventory, select the item and press Scan with PDA. The button is only there while the PDA is in a hand.

Files

Your computer has two folders.

  • guide/ holds pages from this guide as text: the page for your job, the pages its work leans on, and the basics. They follow the job you hold.
  • home/ holds files you write yourself: up to {{ d.MAX_OWN_FILES }} of them, each up to {{ d.MAX_FILE_CHARS }} characters. A new career or round starts with home.md, a sample that shows how to format a file.
  • A file whose name ends in .md is shown formatted: headings, lists, bold, italic and tables. Other files are shown as plain text.
  • A link to another page of this guide opens that page's file, when it is in your guide folder. Back returns to the page the link was on, and Close returns to the Files list.
  • The computer in your quarters reads every file, and makes, edits and deletes your own: Edit is at the top of an open file, and Delete is under it and beside each file in the Files list. Guide files can only be read.
  • The PDA can only read them.
  • In a career your files come with you from shift to shift. In a single round they last for the round. See {{ w.link("Modes and Careers") }}.
