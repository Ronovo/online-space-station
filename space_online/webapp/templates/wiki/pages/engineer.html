What you do

You keep the station powered. From the Engineering Panel you watch the battery, switch the solar array, set each system's level and pick the power mode. See {{ w.link("Power") }}. You can also lock and unlock the Engineering Bay door.

The Combustion Engine is yours to run: it is the station's main power, and the solar array is only a backup. When you're the Engineer, the engine starts unlit and the solar array switched off, so lighting the engine is your first job. Then keep it fed with canisters from Atmospherics and tuned to what is in them. See {{ w.link("Engineering") }}.

Job events

  • Solar Panels Offline: the array shuts down. Switch it back on from the Engineering Panel. It doesn't happen again within {{ facts.events.SOLAR_TRIP_COOLDOWN_SECONDS // 60 }} minutes.
  • System Fault: one of {{ facts.faultable|join(", ") }} faults and shuts off. Carry Basic Tools or a Repair Tool and press Repair on the panel. See {{ w.link("Engineering") }}.

Room hazards aren't job events: the station rolls them on its own, and they are yours to fix. See {{ w.link("Hazards") }}.

With you in the job, nobody else restarts the array, repairs a fault, fixes a hazard or runs the engine. A dead battery also takes life support down, and bringing it back up once there is charge is yours too.

Tips

  • Keep Basic Tools from your locker on you, so a fault, a breach or bad wiring can be fixed straight away, and make a Fire Extinguisher at the fabricator, under Room Options in the Engineering Bay, for fires.
  • Keep life support above level {{ facts.air.KEEPS_UP_LEVEL|int }}, or the air thins out and the whole crew starts losing oxygen. See {{ w.link("Life Support") }}.
  • Your PDA's Power Readout shows the battery, the engine's output, the air and the reservoir. It messages you about faults, hazards, a failing battery, a damaged combustion chamber, bad air and a low reservoir. See {{ w.link("PDA") }}.
  • Keep an eye on the engine's hint line. A cut coolant line overheats a running engine: repair it before the chamber gives out.
