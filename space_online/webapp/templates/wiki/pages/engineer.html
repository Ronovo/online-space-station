What you do

You keep the station powered. From the Engineering Panel you watch the battery, switch the solar array, set each system's level and pick the power mode. See {{ w.link("Power") }}. You can also lock and unlock the Engineering Bay door.

When you're the Engineer, the solar array starts switched off, so turning it on is your first job.

Job events

  • Solar Panels Offline: the array shuts down. Switch it back on from the Engineering Panel.
  • System Fault: {{ facts.faultable|join(", ") }} faults and shuts off. Carry Basic Tools or a Repair Tool and press Repair on the panel. See {{ w.link("Engineering") }}.

Tips

  • Keep Basic Tools from your locker on you, so a fault can be fixed straight away.
  • Keep life support above level {{ facts.oxygen.DAMAGE_THRESHOLD_LEVEL }}, or the whole crew starts losing oxygen. See {{ w.link("Life Support") }}.
